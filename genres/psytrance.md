# Psytrance Lighting Genre

## Overview

Psytrance is the most visually **psychedelic** and **technically intensive** EDM subgenre. It originated in Goa, India, and defines itself through UV/blacklight dominance, sacred geometry gobos, fractal-mimicking prism effects, and continuous high-energy atmosphere that never truly breaks down. The lighting aesthetic should feel like a controlled hallucination: hypnotic, alien, transcendent, and relentless. UV-reactive fluorescence, neon saturation, and constant gobo/prism rotation are not optional — they are foundational to the genre's identity.

Unlike house, techno, or drum & bass, psytrance has **no traditional drop structure**. Energy is relentlessly building, with brief atmospheric respites that still maintain momentum. Lighting reflects this: movement is perpetual, strobes peak during intensity climaxes, and the color palette stays locked in the neon/UV spectrum throughout the set.

## BPM Range

- **Full-on Psytrance**: 145–150 BPM (the standard)
- **Progressive Psy**: 136–140 BPM (deeper, more hypnotic)
- **Dark Psytrance**: 148–155 BPM (harder, more intense)
- **Psychill/Ambient Psy**: 100–120 BPM (atmospheric intro/outro material)

**Default for genre**: 145 BPM

## Energy Profile

| Section | BPM | Duration | Energy | Intensity | Characteristics |
|---------|-----|----------|--------|-----------|-----------------|
| Intro/Build | 145 | 8–16 bars | 60% | Atmospheric | Slow gobo rotation, purple base, minimal strobes, haze dominant |
| Rolling Groove | 145 | 16–32 bars | 75% | Hypnotic | Green/purple layering, continuous gobo rotation, light strobe, mover circles |
| Intensification | 145 | 16–32 bars | 85% | Escalating | Faster gobo rot, dual prism engage, pink accents, strobe medium, neon saturation |
| Peak Plateau | 145 | 16–24 bars | 95% | Maximal | Full prism cascade, laser full RGB, UV dominant, strobe fast, all movement full speed |
| Brief Respite | 145 | 4–8 bars | 75% | Recalibration | Gobo slow, one prism only, teal/cyan accent, strobe drops to light |
| Higher Peak | 145 | 16–24 bars | 98% | Transcendent | Faster than previous peak, laser density high, strobes max, continuous rotation on all effects |

**Key difference from other genres**: Energy is **always climbing**. The "respite" sections are NOT breakdowns — they are brief atmospheric catches before the next ascent. There is no rest, only rhythm.

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors (3)

1. **Neon Green** — The signature psytrance color. Hyperactive, alien, hypnotic.
   - RGB: (0, 255, 0)
   - BSW: Green (38)
   - Sharpy: Green (40)
   - Profile: Green (20)

2. **Deep Purple** — Sacred, transcendent, otherworldly. The complement to green.
   - RGB: (100, 0, 255)
   - BSW: Magenta (50, shifted cool)
   - Sharpy: Purple (50)
   - Profile: Magenta blend (30 + 15 = teal-shifted)

3. **UV Blue** — Blacklight reinforcer. Pure, glowing, ethereal.
   - RGB: (0, 0, 255)
   - BSW: Blue (44)
   - Sharpy: Blue (30)
   - Profile: Blue (15)

### Accent Colors (3)

1. **Neon Pink** — Secondary psychedelic color. UV-reactive, high saturation.
   - RGB: (255, 0, 200)
   - BSW: Pink (62)
   - Sharpy: Pink (60)
   - Profile: Pink (30)

2. **Cyan** — Icy, futuristic counterpoint. Complements orange heat.
   - RGB: (0, 255, 255)
   - BSW: Teal (56)
   - Sharpy: Teal (70)
   - Profile: Teal (35)

3. **Orange** — Festival fire, warmth against cool dominance. Grounding color.
   - RGB: (255, 100, 0)
   - BSW: Orange (26)
   - Sharpy: Orange (90)
   - Profile: Orange (25)

### Two-Color Pairs (Movement Layers)

1. **Green ↔ Purple** — Primary hypnotic cycle. Never drop this pair from peaks.
2. **Purple ↔ Pink** — Psychedelic intensity. Use during climax sections.
3. **Green ↔ Cyan** — Cool ethereal wash. Use for respite/atmospheric moments.
4. **Pink ↔ Orange** — Warm psychedelic. Festival energy, less common, use for accents.
5. **Blue ↔ Cyan** — Cold laser pairs. For NI3K laser patterns and aerial effects.
6. **Purple ↔ Orange** — Contrasting heat/cool. Use sparingly, dramatic moments only.

### Avoid

- **Reds alone** — Psytrance is alien, not aggressive/angry. Pure red feels more techno/dark techno.
- **Yellow** — Clashes with green saturation. Psytrance doesn't use warm yellows.
- **White/Cool white wash** — Kills the UV aesthetic. Use only as a transitional flash or as a laser base.
- **Muted/pastel colors** — All colors must be neon-saturated. Psytrance is hyperactive visually.
- **Monochrome sections** — Always layer min 2 colors. Green alone = boring. Purple alone = dark techno. Green + Purple = psytrance.

## Movement Conventions

### Rolling Groove (Verse, 75% energy)
- **Movers**: Slow continuous circles (radius ~30° pan, ~20° tilt) around center stage. Think orbital paths.
- **Speed**: Smooth, hypnotic — one full orbit per 4–8 beats.
- **Pattern**: All movers trace the same circle in parallel (synchronized orbits).
- **Color layering**: Alternate green ↔ purple every 2–4 bars. No strobes, gentle gobo rotate.

### Intensification (Build, 85% energy)
- **Movers**: Circles accelerate. Orbital period drops to 2–4 beats. Add figure-8 weaves.
- **Speed**: Smooth, but snappier transitions. Movers begin to "cross" center.
- **Pattern**: Two orbits per section — one per pair (sharpy + profile as one group, BSW/NI3K as second).
- **Color layering**: Pink and cyan accents introduced. Gobo rotation speed increases (0.5 rev per 4 bars → 0.5 rev per 2 bars).

### Peak Plateau (Drop equivalent, 95%+ energy)
- **Movers**: Aggressive continuous rotation. Pan sweeps 0°–360° over 2–3 beats. Tilt oscillates 20°–80° continuously.
- **Speed**: Maximum smooth speed — no holds, pure motion.
- **Pattern**: All movers rotate independently but in sync — each one spins on its own axis while the group traces expanding/contracting spirals.
- **Laser effects**: Full RGB on NI3K. Aerial patterns. Laser strobe during beat hits.
- **Color layering**: Green + Purple dominant, pink laser accents, full brightness.

### Brief Respite (Atmospheric catch, 75% energy)
- **Movers**: Slow down to single-orbit paths. One fixture per position (e.g., sharpy center, BSW DSL, profile USR).
- **Speed**: Very slow smooth — one orbit per 8–16 beats.
- **Pattern**: Movers "settle" into safe zones (center, front-left, front-right). Minimal crossing.
- **Color layering**: Shift to cool pair (green ↔ cyan or blue ↔ cyan). Gobo slow, one prism only.

### Higher Peak (Climax, 98%+ energy)
- **Movers**: Return to aggressive rotation but FASTER than previous peak. Pan 0°–360° in 1–2 beats. Tilt bounces 10°–90°.
- **Speed**: Maximum.
- **Pattern**: Synchronized multi-fixture spirals. All movers trace inward → outward → inward over 8 beats.
- **Laser effects**: NI3K lasers at maximum speed and density. RGB laser patterns every beat.
- **Color layering**: Back to hot pair (purple ↔ pink or green ↔ pink). Full saturation, full brightness, full strobe.

## Timing Presets (at 145 BPM)

| Duration | Bars | FadeIn (ms) | Hold (ms) | Total (ms) | Use Case |
|----------|------|-------------|----------|-----------|----------|
| 1 beat | 0.25 | 414 | 0 | 414 | Laser hit, strobe accent |
| 2 beats | 0.5 | 828 | 0 | 828 | Mover snap, quick color shift |
| 4 beats (1 bar) | 1 | 1656 | 0 | 1656 | Gobo introduction, gentle color change |
| 8 beats (2 bars) | 2 | 3312 | 0 | 3312 | Mover orbit cycle, smooth color pair fade |
| 16 beats (4 bars) | 4 | 6624 | 0 | 6624 | Section transition, full intensity ramp |
| 32 beats (8 bars) | 8 | 13248 | 0 | 13248 | Intro/outro sequences, atmospheric builds |
| Snap on beat hit | — | 0 | 207 | 207 | Strobe sync, laser flash, prism on/off |
| Slow orbit | — | 0 | 3312 | 3312 | Mover sustain per 4 beats |

**Key principle**: Psytrance uses mostly **smooth transitions with no holds** (FadeIn = total duration, Hold = 0). The entire section IS the motion. Snaps are reserved for laser strobes and prism toggles on beat hits.

## Strobe Conventions

Psytrance strobes are **peak-oriented and UV-synced**. They are NOT constant — they surge with energy.

### Rolling Groove (75% energy)
- **Shutter/Strobe**: Off or minimal. If used, very slow strobe (Ch6 = 100–120 on Sharpy, Ch16 = 40–60 on BSW).
- **Purpose**: Atmosphere, not pulse. The rotating gobos carry the rhythm.

### Intensification (85% energy)
- **Shutter/Strobe**: Medium strobe. Sharpy Ch6 = 140–170. BSW Ch16 = 80–110.
- **Sync**: Every 2 beats (half-bar strobe bursts).

### Peak Plateau (95%+ energy)
- **Shutter/Strobe**: FAST. Sharpy Ch6 = 180–210 (approaching open). BSW Ch16 = 120–131 (fastest before sustained).
- **Sync**: Every beat or double-time. Create a "stutter" effect: 1 beat strobe, 1 beat open, repeat.
- **UV strobe concept**: If venue has blacklight, white strobes under blacklight simulate UV strobe (neon colors glow extra bright in stutter).

### Brief Respite (75% energy)
- **Shutter/Strobe**: OFF or very slow (Sharpy 100, BSW 40). Movers settling visual, not strobed.

### Higher Peak (98%+ energy)
- **Shutter/Strobe**: Max strobe rate. Sharpy Ch6 = 200–220. BSW Ch16 = 125–131. NI3K laser strobe every beat.
- **Sync**: Double-time strobe (2 strobes per beat). Create a seizure-safe but intense pulse.

**Golden Rule**: Psytrance strobes should feel like a **hypnotic heartbeat**, not an aggressive assault. They sync to the groove and build with energy.

## Laser Conventions

Psytrance is the **second-most laser-heavy genre** (after trance). Lasers are treated as primary effect, not accent.

### Always-On Baseline
- **NI3K Red Laser**: Channel 14, set to strobe mode (values 16–249 for strobe, 250–255 for on). During peaks, cycle strobe fast (16–30) for pulse, switch to on (250–255) during climax.
- **NI3K Green Laser**: Same timing as red, offset by 1–2 beats for pattern variety.
- **NI3K Blue Laser**: Activate during higher peaks only. Creates full RGB aerial patterns.

### Peak Patterns (95%+ energy)
- **Single color per beat**: Red on beat 1, green on beat 3, blue on beat 2+4, then all three on beat 4 → repeat.
- **Aerial sweeps**: Pan and tilt NI3K continuously (same as mover rotation). Lasers trace circles and spirals through the air.
- **Color pairs**: Green ↔ Red (complementary), Blue ↔ Green (cool), all three (climax white aerial).

### Brief Respite
- **Laser off** or single color strobe (red only, slow). Reduces visual intensity.

### Higher Peak
- **All three lasers on**, max intensity, strobe every beat, continuous mover motion carrying aerial patterns across venue.

**Key effect**: Laser tilt should follow mover tilt. If movers sweep 0°–80° tilt, NI3K lasers do the same, creating a "laser beam" effect that mirrors the light movement.

## Gobo & Prism Conventions

Gobos and prisms are **visual pillars** of psytrance. They are never off during peaks — only during intro/respite.

### Gobo Rotation Speeds
- **Intro** (60% energy): Off or index position (no rotation). Sacred geometry static.
- **Rolling Groove** (75% energy): Slow rotation (0.5 rev per 4 bars). Meditative mandala effect.
- **Intensification** (85% energy): Medium rotation (0.5 rev per 2 bars). Hypnotic acceleration.
- **Peak Plateau** (95% energy): Fast rotation (1 rev per 1–2 bars). Fractal dizziness.
- **Brief Respite**: Gobo slow or off. Return to static mandala.
- **Higher Peak**: Gobo max speed (1 rev per 0.5–1 bar). Maximum fractal psychedelia.

### Preferred Gobo Patterns
- **Sacred geometry**: Triangles, mandalas, spirals, hexagons (if available in fixture library). These evoke tribal/spiritual psytrance origins.
- **Breakup patterns**: Anything that fractures light into multiple beams. Psytrance loves geometric fragmentation.
- **Avoid**: Abstract organic shapes (leaves, clouds). Too natural for psytrance's alien aesthetic.

### Prism Engagement
- **One prism on** during Rolling Groove and Intensification sections. Adds beam multiplication without chaos.
- **Two prisms on** during Peak Plateau. Creates "cascade" effect: one prism multiplies beams horizontally (pan), second multiplies vertically (tilt).
- **Prism rotation**: Enable prism rotation during peaks. Sharpy Ch12 = 200 (spinning). BSW Ch14 = 128–189 (CCW fast→slow).
- **Respite**: One prism off, second prism slow rotation or off.

## Par Behavior

4BAR and Missyee RGB LED pars are **secondary** to movers/lasers but essential for **ambient wash cohesion**.

### Rolling Groove (75% energy)
- **Pattern**: All pars same color — purple base wash. Creates unified, hypnotic background.
- **Dimmer**: 60–80% intensity. Movers are the focus.

### Intensification (85% energy)
- **Pattern**: Pairs: P1+P3 = purple, P2+P4 = green. Alternating every 2 bars. Missyees split: one pair cyan, one pair pink.
- **Dimmer**: 75–90% intensity. More active than rolling groove.

### Peak Plateau (95%+ energy)
- **Pattern**: All pars cycling through green → purple → pink → cyan over 4 beats. Maximum color churn.
- **Dimmer**: 95–100% intensity. Full brightness.
- **Strobe sync**: Optional — par strobe every 2 beats during max strobe sections (adds extra visual density).

### Brief Respite (75% energy)
- **Pattern**: All pars same color — cyan or blue base wash. Cool, settling aesthetic.
- **Dimmer**: 50–70% intensity. Dimmed to emphasize mover respite.

### Higher Peak (98%+ energy)
- **Pattern**: Gradient wash: P1 green → P2 cyan → P3 purple → P4 pink. Smooth rainbow across stage. Missyees add strobe (every beat) on top.
- **Dimmer**: 100% intensity. Max brightness.

**Philosophy**: Pars are the **ambient glue** that holds the psychedelic color palette together. They should never fight movers — when movers are active (peaks), pars provide a unified wash; when movers settle (respite), pars shift color to mark the transition.

## Fixture Layering

### Primary Movers (movement + color + effects)
1. **Sharpy Knockoff** (ID 8, ch 6 strobe, ch 8 color, ch 10 gobo) — Fast pan/tilt, essential for circular motion
2. **Profile Knockoff** (ID 4, ch 1 strobe, ch 5 color, ch 6 gobo) — Complementary pan/tilt, secondary circles
3. **BSW 3-in-1** (ID 1, ch 16 shutter, ch 8 color, ch 9–11 gobos) — Heavy beam shape, slow rotation effects
4. **NI3K Nausea Inducer** (ID 3, ch 0 pan, ch 1–3 tilt, ch 6 strobe, ch 14–16 lasers) — Laser domination + aerial effects

### Secondary Wash (ambient color + cohesion)
5. **Chauvet 4BAR** (ID 2, 4 RGB pars) — Par wash, color pairs, saturation layer
6. **Missyee 36 RGB LED** (ID 5–6, RGB split) — Fine color accent, shadow fill, RGB cycling

### Fixture Priority During Peaks
- **Movers first**: All movers at max speed, rotation, strobe.
- **NI3K lasers second**: Full RGB pattern, aerial sweeps, every-beat strobe.
- **Pars third**: Gradient or cycling color, high dimmer, synchronized to mover beats.
- **Gobo/prism rotation**: Max speed, all active, no holds.

### Dimmer Hierarchy (Peak Moment)
- Movers: 100% → 90% (ensure gobo projection clarity)
- NI3K: 100% (lasers independent of dimmer, always full laser power)
- 4BAR: 100%
- Missyees: 100%
- All shutters/strobes: Open or strobe (not dimmed)

## Haze & Atmosphere

Psytrance **demands haze** to show laser beams, gobo projections, and beam multiplication effects. Without haze, the genre loses 40% of its impact.

### Haze Timing
- **Intro**: Light haze (20–30% hazer output). Moody, atmospheric gobo visible.
- **Rolling Groove**: Medium haze (40–50%). Gobo projection soft, readable.
- **Intensification**: Heavy haze (60–70%). Gobo rotation and prism effects become visible.
- **Peak Plateau**: Max haze (80–100%). Laser beams clearly visible in air. Prism cascade creates visible light rays.
- **Brief Respite**: Medium haze (40%). Tone down intensity.
- **Higher Peak**: Max haze (90–100%) + occasional haze burst on beat hits. Create "fog glow" moments.

### Special Haze Moments
- **Haze pulse on beat**: Every 16 beats, hit the hazer for 2–3 seconds. Creates a "fog burst" visual that syncs with music hits.
- **Laser haze**: Always ensure heavy haze when NI3K lasers are active. Aerial beams should glow.
- **Strobe + haze**: Strobes in heavy haze create a pulsing fog effect (safe, visual, no seizure risk if haze diffuses the strobe).

## Venue Scale Notes

Psytrance scales from **intimate clubs** to **massive outdoor festivals**. Lighting design adjusts:

### Small Venue (100–300 capacity, 10m x 10m stage)
- **Fixture load**: 1–2 movers (sharpy + profile), NI3K, 4BAR, 1–2 missyees, basic hazer.
- **Focus**: NI3K lasers become CRITICAL (only laser element). Heavy gobo/prism rotation on movers.
- **Par cohesion**: All 4BAR pars visible, missyees fill gaps.
- **Movement**: Circular orbits on a smaller stage feel hypnotic even at low BPM.

### Medium Venue (500–1500 capacity, 15m x 15m stage)
- **Fixture load**: 2–3 movers, full NI3K + BSW, full 4BAR, 2 missyees, industrial hazer (1500W+).
- **Focus**: Balanced mover + laser + par interplay. All elements visible from any position.
- **Movement**: Larger orbits, figure-8 weaves, full pan/tilt range utilized.

### Large Festival Stage (3000+ capacity, 20m+ wide)
- **Fixture load**: 4+ movers (sharpy pairs, profile pairs, BSW pair), NI3K, full 4BAR array, 4+ missyees, multiple hazers (distributed).
- **Focus**: Movers dominate distance, NI3K lasers visible from back-of-crowd, pars create foreground wash.
- **Movement**: Max pan/tilt range, continuous synchronized rotation across all fixtures.

**Default assumption**: Medium venue (500–1500 capacity). Scale up gobo speed and pan/tilt range for large; scale down for intimate.

- **Gobo mandala projection**: Sacred geometry rotating slowly during grooves, fast during peaks. Hypnotic effect.

## Key Philosophy

Psytrance lighting is **psychedelic first, aggressive second**. The goal is not to assault the audience with strobes (like dark techno) but to create a sustained, hypnotic trance state where the audience feels the music visually.

- **Color saturation is sacred**: Green + Purple must feel **vivid and alien**, never muted.
- **Movement is perpetual**: Even in "quiet" moments, something must be rotating (gobo, prism, mover). Stillness breaks the trance.
- **Lasers are mandatory at scale**: If NI3K is available, it must be used for aerial effects and color patterns.
- **Haze is infrastructure**: Without haze, psytrance lighting is incomplete. It transforms gobos into visible projections and makes laser beams glow.
- **Energy is climbing, never descending**: Every section builds on the last. "Respite" moments are brief — 4–8 bars max — before the next ascent.
- **Sacred geometry aesthetics**: Psytrance origins are spiritual (Goa, India, trance rituals). Fractal gobos and prism cascades evoke this. Use geometric patterns, not organic ones.
- **Festival mindset**: Design for outdoor stages, multi-hour sets, and audiences dancing in trance states. Lighting should feel like part of a larger psychedelic experience, not a conventional show.
