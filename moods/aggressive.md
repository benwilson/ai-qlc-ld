# Mood: Aggressive

## Overview

Angry, intense, in-your-face. Light should feel like an assault — rapid changes, high
contrast, heavy strobe, maximum color saturation. The audience should feel the energy
physically. Think mosh pit, headbanging, walls of sound translated to walls of light.

Use when: heavy drops, peak energy moments, hard-hitting sets, any genre pushed to its
most intense extreme. Excision-style visual violence.

## Category

emotional

## Visual References

- Excision's stage during a bass drop — strobes, lasers, pure visual violence
- A volcanic eruption at night — molten red against black, explosive force
- The final boss fight in a video game — everything at once, sensory overload

## Color Scheme

- **Type**: complementary
- **Rationale**: Opposing colors (red/cyan, orange/blue) create maximum visual tension
  and contrast, matching the mood's aggressive intensity.

## Color Filtering

- **Temperature bias**: Hot. Prefer warm and intense colors — reds, oranges, magentas.
- **Saturation preference**: Maximum. Full saturation, no pastels, no subtlety.
- **Palette selection**: From the genre's two-color pairs, prefer pairs with red, white,
  or any high-contrast combination. Avoid pairs with muted or cool colors.
- **Preferred colors**: Red, white, orange, magenta. Green only for "toxic" looks.
  Avoid blues and teals (too calm).
- **Max simultaneous colors**: 1–2. Aggressive mood is bold and simple — one or two colors
  hitting hard, not a nuanced palette.
- **Par behavior override**: Pars at full intensity. `par_wash()` for unified color blast
  or `par_pairs()` alternating with beats for rhythmic punch.

## Intensity Modifiers

- **Base brightness**: 80% of genre default. Bright and relentless.
- **Contrast ratio**: Maximum. Blackout → full blast transitions. No gradual changes.
- **Blackout frequency**: High. Blackouts are a weapon — brief blackout → instant full
  creates the biggest impact. Use 1-beat blackouts as rhythmic accents.
- **Number of fixtures active**: Maximum during peaks. All fixtures at full during drops.
  But use single-fixture moments for brief tension before unleashing everything.
- **Fixture density**: Dense — most fixtures active, creating overwhelming visual intensity.
  Use all available fixtures simultaneously during drop moments.
- **Dimmer behavior**: 80–100% at all times. Aggressive mood doesn't do "subtle." Even
  breakdowns run at 50–60%.
- **Key-to-fill ratio**: 4:1 — bright but not exclusively directional. Multiple key lights
  create layered aggression rather than a single focal point.

## Movement Modifiers

- **Speed multiplier**: 1.5x genre default. Fast, aggressive movements.
- **Width preference**: Maximum. Movers at extreme positions — full room coverage. Wide
  spreads and full sweeps.
- **Movement style**: Angular, snappy, and violent. Hard position changes with zero fade.
  No smooth curves — straight lines and sharp angles. Rapid position changes create a sense of
  physical assault.
- **Position hold time**: 0.5x genre default. Rapid position changes — use 0.25x during peak moments.
  Don't let anything sit still. Movement IS the aggression.
- **Preferred positions**: Audience Blinder (with strobe), extreme spread positions,
  cross patterns. Avoid static center holds (too passive). Movers should attack the audience from
  multiple angles simultaneously.

## Effect Density

- **Strobe**: Heavy. Full-rig strobe during drops, mover strobe during builds. Strobe is
  the primary effect in aggressive mood.
- **Lasers**: Full. All NI3K lasers on during peaks. Laser strobe for the biggest moments.
- **Gobos**: Yes. Tight gobos for defined, sharp beams. Gobo rotation at high speed during
  drops.
- **Prism**: Yes. Dual prism spinning for maximum chaos. Prism + gobo + strobe = aggressive
  signature look.
- **Frost**: Never. Soft light has no place in aggressive mood. Hard, defined beams only.
- **Haze**: Medium-heavy — enough for beam visibility, not so thick it softens the look.
  Aggressive mood demands sharp, punchy beams visible through controlled atmosphere.
- **Visual assault technique**: Brief moments of ALL fixtures at maximum intensity + strobe + lasers,
  followed by snap blackout. This 2–4 beat pattern (full blast → darkness) creates maximum physical impact.

## Timing Modifiers

- **Fade speed**: 0.25x genre default (much faster fades — near instant). Snap changes.
- **Exception**: None. Everything is instant or near-instant.
- **Hold duration**: 0.5x genre default. Don't hold anything long — keep it moving.
- **Chase speed**: 2x genre default. Fast chases, fast changes, fast everything.

## Energy Curve

- **Baseline**: High. Aggressive mood starts high and stays high.
- **Peak scaling**: 120% of genre peak. Push beyond the genre's normal maximum.
- **Rise time**: Fast. Energy ramps up in 4–8 bars, not 16.
- **Fall time**: Instant. Energy drops are cliff-edge — full to blackout in 1 beat.
- **Sustain**: Short at any single level. Aggressive mood is constantly escalating or
  crashing.

## Phases (Optional)

### Phase 1: Pounding
- **Duration**: 8–16 bars
- **Character**: Relentless, sustained aggression. Every beat hits hard.
- **Modifier adjustments**: Full intensity, fast snaps, strobe on, all effects engaged.

### Phase 2: Brutal Hit
- **Duration**: 1–2 bars
- **Character**: A single devastating moment — blackout to full white blinder strobe.
- **Modifier adjustments**: 1-beat blackout → full rig + strobe + lasers for 2–4 beats.

## Genre Interaction Notes

- **DnB**: Natural pairing for jump-up, neurofunk, and heavy DnB. Amplify drop behavior,
  reduce breakdown duration, faster movement on rolls. Position hold time at 0.25x for relentless
  fixture cycling.
- **Dubstep**: Perfect match. Dubstep + Aggressive = Excision-tier visual assault. Push
  all dubstep conventions to maximum — more strobe, faster wobble sync, more lasers.
- **House**: Unusual — works for peak-time tech house or hard house. Push the groove into
  pounding territory. Increase strobe usage beyond house norms. Rapid par flashing creates the
  aggressive quality.
- **Techno**: Works for hard techno, industrial techno. Push the minimal aesthetic toward
  overwhelming. More strobe, faster pulse, red-heavy palette. Strobe becomes the primary effect.
- **Trance**: Works for hard trance and psytrance. Suppress the emotional/beautiful aspects,
  amplify the driving energy. More strobe, sharper movements. Use aggressive intensity to destroy
  euphoria.
- **Melodic Techno**: Possible but unusual — suppress the "melodic" quality with heavy strobing
  and relentless laser fire. White + red only, minimize movement for raw aggression.
- **Psytrance**: Excellent match for hard psytrance. Rapid gobo rotation + strobe creates visual
  disorientation matching sonic assault. Green + red palette at maximum saturation.
- **Liquid DnB**: Contrary pairing but possible for "aggressive liquid" moments. Suppress smooth
  fades entirely, rapid color changes, higher fixture density to overwhelm the genre's fluidity.
- **Future Bass**: Interesting contrast — take the genre's neon palette and add violent strobe,
  rapid movement, and all-fixtures-active moments. Creates high-energy, explosive aesthetic.
- **Hardstyle**: Perfect match. Hardstyle's kick focus demands aggressive lighting. Red + white
  strobing synced to the kick, rapid mover cycling, visual punch matching the beat's violence.
