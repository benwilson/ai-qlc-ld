# Mood: Building

## Overview

Rising tension, progressive layering, anticipation. Light should feel like it's gathering
energy — starting sparse and accumulating, each new element adding to the sense of
inevitability. The audience should feel the anticipation physically, knowing something big
is coming. Think the 32 bars before a massive drop, the rollercoaster climbing the first hill.

Use when: builds before drops, progressive sections, rising tension anywhere in a set,
the approach to a climax.

## Category

energy

## Visual References

- A rollercoaster climbing the first hill — the ratcheting anticipation
- Storm clouds gathering on the horizon — the pressure building before the break
- A rocket countdown — systems engaging one by one, energy accumulating

## Color Scheme

- **Type**: analogous → complementary (progressive)
- **Rationale**: Start with harmonious analogous colors (calm, unified), progressively shift
  toward complementary contrast as tension builds. The color scheme itself escalates.

## Color Filtering

- **Temperature bias**: Starts cool, shifts warm. The color temperature should rise with
  the energy — blues at the start, shifting toward magentas/reds/whites as the build peaks.
- **Saturation preference**: Rising. Start desaturated, end fully saturated. The colors
  "intensify" along with the energy.
- **Palette selection**: Use a progression through the genre's palette. Start with the
  coolest/darkest pair, transition through mid-tones, arrive at the warmest/brightest
  pair by the peak.
- **Preferred colors**: Start with deep blues/purples, transition through teals/cyans, arrive
  at whites/magentas/warm colors.
- **Max simultaneous colors**: Increasing. Start with 1, end with 2–3. Layer in new colors
  as the build progresses.
- **Par behavior override**: Start pars off. Add pars one at a time as the build progresses.
  By the peak, all pars at full in the arrival color.

## Intensity Modifiers

- **Base brightness**: Progressive. Start at 30% of genre default, end at 90%.
- **Contrast ratio**: Increasing. Start with low contrast, end with high contrast (to set
  up the dramatic payoff at the drop).
- **Blackout frequency**: None during the build itself. But the BUILD should end with a
  setup for whatever comes next (often a dramatic blackout → explosion).
- **Number of fixtures active**: Progressive. Start with 1 fixture, add one every 4–8 bars.
  By the peak, most or all fixtures are active.
- **Dimmer behavior**: Ramping. Gradually increase from 30% to 90% over the build duration.

## Movement Modifiers

- **Speed multiplier**: Progressive. Start at 0.5x genre default, end at 1.5x. Movement
  accelerates through the build.
- **Width preference**: Progressive. Start narrow (movers at center), end wide (movers
  spread to full width). The room "opens up" as energy builds.
- **Movement style**: Start smooth, end snappier. The transition from smooth to angular
  mirrors the musical build from flowing to pounding.
- **Position hold time**: Decreasing. Start with 2x genre default, end with 0.5x. Changes
  come faster and faster.
- **Preferred positions**: Center (start) → Spread (mid) → Wide (end). Use the Back-Front
  sweep path or Converge path — movements that feel like they're building toward something.

## Effect Density

- **Strobe**: Accelerating. No strobe at the start. Add slow strobe partway through. Increase
  to fast strobe in the final 2–4 bars. The strobe acceleration IS the build.
- **Lasers**: Progressive. Off at start. Single color laser at midpoint. Add more laser colors
  approaching the peak. Full lasers right before the drop.
- **Gobos**: Progressive. Open beam at start. Add gobo texture at midpoint. The gobo adds
  visual complexity as energy rises.
- **Prism**: Progressive. Off at start. Add prism in the final 4–8 bars. Prism is a
  "penultimate" effect that signals the climax is near.
- **Frost**: Start frosted (soft), end open (hard). The beams "sharpen" as the build peaks.
- **Haze**: Medium-heavy. Builds look best with clear beam visibility.

## Timing Modifiers

- **Fade speed**: Decreasing. Start at 2x genre default (slow fades), end at 0.5x (fast snaps).
  Transitions accelerate through the build.
- **Exception**: The final 2–4 bars should use the genre's fastest timing preset.
- **Hold duration**: Decreasing. Start at 2x, end at 0.5x. Changes come faster and faster.
- **Chase speed**: Accelerating. If chases are running, their speed increases through the
  build. This is the classic "accelerating strobe" or "accelerating par chase" technique.

## Energy Curve

- **Baseline**: Rising. This mood IS a rising energy curve.
- **Peak scaling**: 95% of genre peak. Building mood gets close to peak but doesn't quite
  arrive — the peak mood (or the drop itself) provides the final payoff.
- **Rise time**: This IS the rise. The entire duration of the building mood is the rise time.
  Typically 8–32 bars depending on genre.
- **Fall time**: N/A — building mood doesn't fall. It either arrives at the peak or the
  operator switches to another mood.
- **Sustain**: None. Building mood never sustains — it's always rising.

## Phases (Optional)

### Phase 1: Gathering
- **Duration**: 8–16 bars
- **Character**: First hints of energy. One fixture, one color, barely noticeable increase.
- **Modifier adjustments**: Base brightness 30%, 1–2 fixtures, cool palette, slow movement.

### Phase 2: Rising
- **Duration**: 8–16 bars
- **Character**: Clearly building now. Fixtures and colors accumulating.
- **Modifier adjustments**: Base brightness 50–60%, 3–4 fixtures, palette warming, speed increasing.

### Phase 3: Cresting
- **Duration**: 4–8 bars
- **Character**: The final push. Everything ramping to near-peak. Strobe acceleration begins.
- **Modifier adjustments**: Base brightness 80%, most fixtures, warm/bright palette, fast movement,
  accelerating strobe in final 2 bars.

## Genre Interaction Notes

- **DnB**: Classic DnB build. Accelerating par chase, movers spreading, strobe acceleration,
  lasers appearing in the last 4 bars. The build takes 8–16 bars.
- **Dubstep**: Slower, more ominous build. Fixtures adding one at a time. Darkness giving
  way to dread. Single laser cutting in. 8–16 bars.
- **House**: Longer, smoother build. Warm colors gradually intensifying. Pars fading up
  slowly. Movers widening. 16–32 bars. More patient than bass genres.
- **Techno**: Subtle build. More about density than brightness. Layers adding slowly.
  Movement tightening rather than spreading. 16–32 bars.
- **Trance**: The longest builds. 16–32 bars of gradual layering. Color evolution from cool
  to warm. The most dramatic "fixture adding" progression. Strobe acceleration in the
  final 4 bars.
