# Mood: Chill

## Overview

Low energy sustained, ambient, background lighting. Light should feel like it's just there —
a comfortable, undemanding presence that doesn't ask for attention. The audience should barely
notice the lighting consciously, but it subtly enhances the atmosphere. Think lounge bar,
pre-set warmup, waiting for the headliner, background ambiance.

Use when: opening DJ sets, ambient interludes, warm-up acts, background music, any moment
where the lighting should support without leading.

## Category

energy

## Visual References

- A hotel lobby at 2am — soft ambient lighting, nobody paying attention to it
- Lo-fi beats YouTube stream aesthetic — calm, muted, background
- Pre-dawn blue light filtering through curtains — barely there, peaceful

## Color Scheme

- **Type**: monochromatic
- **Rationale**: Minimal color variation keeps the lighting unobtrusive. One soft hue at
  low intensity disappears into the background — which is exactly the goal.

## Color Filtering

- **Temperature bias**: Warm-to-neutral. Gentle, non-demanding colors.
- **Saturation preference**: Low. Muted, pastel-like tones. Desaturate everything by mixing
  in white. Nothing should grab attention.
- **Palette selection**: From the genre's two-color pairs, use only the softest, most
  "background" pair. Avoid any high-contrast or intense combination.
- **Preferred colors**: Muted ambers, soft blues, desaturated teals, warm whites, pale
  lavender. Nothing bright or electric.
- **Max simultaneous colors**: 1–2. Simple, quiet color story.
- **Par behavior override**: Pars at 20–30% intensity. `par_wash()` in a single muted color.
  Pars provide the ambient fill that defines chill — soft, low-key, everywhere.

## Intensity Modifiers

- **Base brightness**: 25% of genre default. Dim. The room is lit enough to navigate, not
  enough to demand visual attention.
- **Contrast ratio**: Minimal. The brightness barely changes. Everything stays in a tight
  20–35% range.
- **Blackout frequency**: None. Too dramatic. Minimum brightness never drops below 15%.
- **Number of fixtures active**: Low. 2–3 fixtures maximum. Pars doing most of the work.
  Movers optional — if used, one at a time at low intensity.
- **Dimmer behavior**: 15–35% range. Barely on. Comfortable background glow.

## Movement Modifiers

- **Speed multiplier**: 0.2x genre default. Glacially slow. Movement should be barely
  perceptible — if you're not watching for it, you wouldn't notice.
- **Width preference**: Narrow. If movers are used, they stay near center with minimal
  travel.
- **Movement style**: Ultra-smooth, near-static. Imperceptible drift. No discernible
  pattern — just gentle wandering.
- **Position hold time**: 4x genre default. Very long holds. Minutes between changes.
- **Preferred positions**: Center only. No dramatic positions. If movers are used at all,
  keep them pointed safely and unobtrusively.

## Effect Density

- **Strobe**: None.
- **Lasers**: None.
- **Gobos**: None. Clean, simple light.
- **Prism**: None.
- **Frost**: Optional. Frost softens movers to create gentle ambient wash.
- **Haze**: Minimal. Just enough for a hint of atmosphere.

## Timing Modifiers

- **Fade speed**: 5x genre default (extremely slow fades). A color change takes 16–32 bars.
  The audience shouldn't notice the change happening.
- **Exception**: None. Everything is slow.
- **Hold duration**: 5x genre default. Extended holds. The look barely changes.
- **Chase speed**: 0.1x genre default or no chases at all.

## Energy Curve

- **Baseline**: Very low. Chill mood is about sustaining minimum energy.
- **Peak scaling**: 30% of genre peak. There are no real "peaks" — just slightly brighter
  moments.
- **Rise time**: Extremely slow (32+ bars).
- **Fall time**: Extremely slow.
- **Sustain**: Maximum. Chill mood is one sustained state with near-imperceptible variation.

## Genre Interaction Notes

- **All genres**: Chill mood overrides most genre-specific conventions. Regardless of genre,
  chill means low, slow, and quiet. DnB at 174 BPM with chill mood still uses slow, muted
  lighting.
- **DnB**: Suppress all drop/breakdown dynamics. Constant low-energy ambient.
- **House**: Most natural pairing. Deep house warmup set = chill mood + house genre.
- **Techno**: Works for ambient techno sets. Even more minimal than techno's already
  minimal conventions.
- **Trance/Dubstep**: Overrides the genre's dynamic conventions entirely. Everything
  stays flat and quiet.
