# Mood: Euphoric

## Overview

Joyful, uplifting, bright, celebratory. Light should feel like pure happiness — wide open,
colorful, expansive. The audience should feel like they're flying, like the whole room is
radiating energy. Think festival mainstage sunrise moment, hands-in-the-air climax, the
biggest smile on the dancefloor.

Use when: peak emotional moments, festival anthems, vocal climaxes, uplifting drops,
the moment the whole crowd loses it together.

## Category

emotional

## Visual References

- Festival mainstage at sunrise — warm light flooding over a sea of raised hands
- Confetti cannon moment — explosion of color and brightness, pure joy
- The final crescendo of a fireworks show — everything blooming at once in the sky

## Color Scheme

- **Type**: triadic
- **Rationale**: Three colors at 120° intervals (like cyan/magenta/yellow or blue/pink/white)
  create rich, vibrant, celebratory looks without feeling chaotic.

## Color Filtering

- **Temperature bias**: Warm-to-bright. Prefer vibrant, luminous colors.
- **Saturation preference**: High but not harsh. Rich, vivid colors — jewel tones rather
  than neon. Think stained glass, not traffic signs.
- **Palette selection**: From the genre's two-color pairs, prefer pairs that feel joyful
  and bright. Blue/cyan, magenta/white, cyan/magenta. Avoid pairs with black/off or
  dark/muted colors.
- **Preferred colors**: White, cyan, magenta, bright blue, bright pink, gold/amber. These
  read as "joy" and "celebration."
- **Max simultaneous colors**: 3+. Euphoric mood is the most colorful mood. Multiple
  colors create a rich, celebratory look. Rainbow moments are acceptable.
- **Par behavior override**: Pars at full intensity in bright, warm colors. `par_wash()` for
  maximum unity, or `par_gradient()` through warm tones. All pars on, always.

## Intensity Modifiers

- **Base brightness**: 90% of genre default. Bright, open, generous with light.
- **Contrast ratio**: Medium. The "dark" moments are still quite bright (60–70%). The
  dynamic range is from "bright" to "very bright," not "dark" to "bright."
- **Blackout frequency**: Rare. Only for the single most impactful moment. Euphoric mood
  keeps the room lit.
- **Number of fixtures active**: Maximum. All fixtures contributing to the look. The whole
  room should glow.
- **Dimmer behavior**: 70–100% range. Even "pulled back" moments stay bright.

## Movement Modifiers

- **Speed multiplier**: 1.2x genre default. Movements feel energetic and alive, but not
  frantic.
- **Width preference**: Maximum. Wide sweeps, full room coverage. The light should fill
  every corner — no shadows.
- **Movement style**: Smooth, expansive arcs. Big, generous movements. Sweeps that feel
  like opening your arms wide. Not angular, not frantic — joyful.
- **Position hold time**: 0.75x genre default. Keep things moving but not rushed.
- **Preferred positions**: Wide spread positions, full sweeps (LR Sweep, Diamond), DSC
  (toward audience). Avoid Ceiling Hit (too inward-looking) and narrow positions.

## Effect Density

- **Strobe**: Moderate. Brief white strobe bursts (2–4 beats) at peak moments feel
  celebratory, not aggressive. Color strobe (cyan, magenta) for extended peaks.
- **Lasers**: Yes. Full RGB laser spread during peaks. Lasers feel magical and exciting in
  euphoric mood, not aggressive. Steady on, not strobing.
- **Gobos**: Minimal. Open beam preferred — clean, bright, expansive. Gobos restrict light
  output, which fights the euphoric brightness.
- **Prism**: Optional. Prism creates multiplied beams which reads as "more light, more
  energy." Single prism with slow rotation.
- **Frost**: Optional. Frosted beams create wide washes that fill the room. Works well on
  some fixtures while others stay tight for definition.
- **Haze**: Medium-heavy. Bright beams through haze create the "beams from heaven" look
  that defines euphoric lighting.

## Timing Modifiers

- **Fade speed**: 0.75x genre default (slightly faster). Transitions should feel energetic
  and responsive, not sluggish.
- **Exception**: During the peak emotional moment, use a single slow 4–8 bar white wash
  reveal — one moment of slow magic before returning to energy.
- **Hold duration**: 0.75x genre default. Keep the energy flowing with frequent changes.
- **Chase speed**: 1.2x genre default. Upbeat, rhythmic chases.

## Energy Curve

- **Baseline**: High. Euphoric mood starts bright and stays bright.
- **Peak scaling**: 110% of genre peak. Push beyond the genre's normal maximum — more
  fixtures, more color, more light.
- **Rise time**: Medium. Energy builds visibly over 8–16 bars.
- **Fall time**: Slow. Euphoric energy lingers — don't kill the mood suddenly.
- **Sustain**: Medium. Euphoric mood needs some variation to stay exciting, but the
  baseline stays high.

## Genre Interaction Notes

- **DnB**: Works for liquid DnB and melodic DnB peaks. Suppress the dark/aggressive drop
  conventions. Bright colors, wide movements, joyful energy even during drops.
- **Dubstep**: Unusual pairing — works for melodic dubstep drops (Illenium style). Replace
  the darkness with brightness, the aggression with exhilaration.
- **House**: Excellent match. House + euphoric = peak-time disco-influenced festival moment.
  Push house's warm palette to its brightest. All pars full, sweeping movers, joyful.
- **Techno**: Challenging — techno's restraint fights euphoric's expressiveness. Can work for
  melodic techno peaks. Let the euphoria override the minimal aesthetic briefly.
- **Trance**: Perfect pairing. This IS what trance lighting is at its best — the main release
  after the breakdown. Full euphoria: wide sweeps, bright colors, all fixtures, lasers,
  hands-in-the-air lighting.
