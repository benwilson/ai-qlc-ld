# Mood: Peak

## Overview

Maximum energy, everything unleashed, climax. This is the moment the entire set has been
building toward — all resources deployed, maximum visual impact. The audience should feel
the full force of the rig. Think the drop hitting, the moment the bass comes back,
hands-in-the-air, the peak of the peak.

Use when: drops, main releases, the 16–32 bars of maximum energy in a set, the moment
you want burned into the audience's memory.

## Category

energy

## Visual References

- The bass drop hitting — the moment the whole room explodes
- A supernova — maximum brightness, maximum energy, radiating outward
- The climax of a fireworks finale — everything at once, sustained brilliance

## Color Scheme

- **Type**: complementary
- **Rationale**: High-contrast complementary colors (the genre's most impactful pair) create
  maximum visual impact at peak energy. Bold, simple, powerful.

## Color Filtering

- **Temperature bias**: Match the genre's peak convention. DnB/dubstep = cold or hot extremes.
  House/trance = warm and bright.
- **Saturation preference**: Maximum. Full saturation, full intensity. Nothing held back.
- **Palette selection**: Use the genre's highest-energy two-color pair. For DnB, that's
  typically the red/white or blue/white pair. For house, magenta/white or cyan/white.
- **Preferred colors**: White (always available at peak), plus the genre's most impactful
  primary color.
- **Max simultaneous colors**: 1–2. Peak moments are bold and simple. One or two colors at
  maximum intensity. Complexity dilutes impact.
- **Par behavior override**: All pars at full intensity. `par_wash()` for unified blast.
  All pars the same bright color for maximum visual unity.

## Intensity Modifiers

- **Base brightness**: 100% of genre default. Full brightness.
- **Contrast ratio**: Maximum. Use blackout beats and full-intensity beats in rapid
  alternation for maximum impact.
- **Blackout frequency**: Strategic. Use 1–2 beat blackouts as rhythmic accents within the
  peak section. Brief darkness makes the light hit harder.
- **Number of fixtures active**: Maximum. All fixtures, all the time. This is the moment
  where everything comes together.
- **Dimmer behavior**: 90–100% range. Full blast. No subtlety.

## Movement Modifiers

- **Speed multiplier**: 1.5x genre default. Fast, impactful movements.
- **Width preference**: Maximum. Full room coverage. Wide sweeps, extreme positions. Fill
  every corner with light.
- **Movement style**: Genre-dependent. DnB/dubstep = fast, angular snaps. House/trance =
  fast, sweeping arcs. But always big, confident movements.
- **Position hold time**: 0.5x genre default. Rapid changes keep the energy high.
- **Preferred positions**: All positions in rapid rotation. Full sweeps, crosses, audience
  blinder (brief). Use the most dramatic sweep paths.

## Effect Density

- **Strobe**: Heavy. Full-rig strobe during the first 2–4 bars of the peak, then vary
  between fixture strobe and no strobe to maintain dynamics within the peak.
- **Lasers**: Full. All NI3K lasers on. This is the moment for full laser deployment.
- **Gobos**: Genre-dependent. DnB/dubstep = tight gobos for defined beams. House/trance =
  open beam for clean brightness.
- **Prism**: Yes. Prism engaged for beam multiplication. Spinning for dynamic visual texture.
- **Frost**: No. Hard, defined beams for maximum visual impact and brightness.
- **Haze**: Heavy. Maximum beam visibility.

## Timing Modifiers

- **Fade speed**: Genre-dependent. DnB/dubstep = 0 (instant snaps). House/trance = 0.5x
  genre default (faster than normal, but not always instant).
- **Exception**: None. Peak is peak — commit fully.
- **Hold duration**: 0.5x genre default. Rapid changes.
- **Chase speed**: 1.5x genre default. Fast chases.

## Energy Curve

- **Baseline**: Maximum. Peak mood IS the maximum.
- **Peak scaling**: 120% of genre peak. Go beyond the genre's normal maximum — more
  fixtures, more effects, more brightness. This is the moment to exceed expectations.
- **Rise time**: Instant. Peak arrives fully formed (the building mood handles the ramp).
- **Fall time**: Varies. Can sustain for 16–32 bars, then either crash (blackout/snap to
  low energy) or gradually reduce.
- **Sustain**: Medium. Peak energy can sustain for the duration of a drop (16–32 bars) but
  needs internal dynamics (varying movement, colors, effects) to stay exciting. Don't
  just hold one static look at max — keep it evolving.

## Internal Dynamics

Peak mood should still have variation within its high-energy state to prevent audience
fatigue. Techniques:

- **Color swap every 4–8 bars**: Same intensity, different palette keeps it fresh.
- **Movement pattern change every 8 bars**: Sweep → cross → spread → sweep.
- **Effect rotation**: Strobe for 4 bars → clean for 4 bars → gobo for 4 bars.
- **Brief blackout accents**: 1-beat blackouts on big drum hits for punctuation.
- **Fixture focus shifts**: Feature different fixture groups for 4-bar stretches —
  mover-heavy → par-heavy → all together.

## Genre Interaction Notes

- **DnB**: The drop. Snap color changes, fast mover sweeps, strobe bursts, full lasers.
  Snap between 2 colors every 2 bars. Maintain for 16–32 bars of the drop.
- **Dubstep**: THE DROP. Maximum chaos but controlled. Wobble-synced color snaps, mover
  sweeps, all strobes, all lasers, all prisms. The most extreme peak of any genre.
- **House**: The peak groove. Less aggressive than bass genres but still full-rig. Sweeping
  movers, full par wash, moderate strobe. Joyful energy rather than aggressive.
- **Techno**: The release. Full rig but still geometric and controlled. White + one color.
  Fast but precise mover movements. Strobe in industrial pulse pattern.
- **Trance**: The main release after the breakdown. Full euphoric peak — wide sweeps, bright
  colors, all fixtures, lasers. The most emotionally satisfying peak.
