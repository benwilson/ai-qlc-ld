# QLC+ Channel Preset Reference

Common channel preset values for use in .qxf fixture definitions.

## Intensity Presets
- `IntensityMasterDimmer` — Master dimmer
- `IntensityDimmer` — Individual dimmer
- `IntensityRed` — Red channel
- `IntensityGreen` — Green channel
- `IntensityBlue` — Blue channel
- `IntensityWhite` — White channel
- `IntensityAmber` — Amber channel
- `IntensityUV` — UV channel
- `IntensityIndigo` — Indigo channel
- `IntensityLime` — Lime channel
- `IntensityCyan` — Cyan channel
- `IntensityMagenta` — Magenta channel

## Position Presets
- `PositionPan` — Pan (coarse)
- `PositionPanFine` — Pan (fine)
- `PositionTilt` — Tilt (coarse)
- `PositionTiltFine` — Tilt (fine)

## Shutter Presets
- `ShutterStrobeSlowFast` — Strobe slow to fast
- `ShutterStrobeFastSlow` — Strobe fast to slow

## Speed Presets
- `SpeedPanSlowFast` — Pan speed
- `SpeedTiltSlowFast` — Tilt speed
- `SpeedPanTiltSlowFast` — Pan/Tilt speed combined

## Color Presets
- `ColorMacro` — Color macro/presets
- `ColorWheelCoarse` — Color wheel (coarse)
- `ColorWheelFine` — Color wheel (fine)
- `ColorCTOMixer` — CTO mixer

## Beam Presets
- `BeamZoomSmallBig` — Zoom small to big
- `BeamZoomBigSmall` — Zoom big to small
- `BeamFocusNearFar` — Focus near to far
- `BeamFocusFarNear` — Focus far to near

## Gobo Presets
- `GoboWheelCoarse` — Gobo wheel (coarse)
- `GoboWheelFine` — Gobo wheel (fine)
- `GoboIndex` — Gobo rotation/index

## Prism Presets
- `PrismRotationSlowFast` — Prism rotation
