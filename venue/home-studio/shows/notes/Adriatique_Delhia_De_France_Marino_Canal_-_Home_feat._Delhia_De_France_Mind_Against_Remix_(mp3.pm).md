# Show Notes: Home (Mind Against Remix)

**Track**: Adriatique, Delhia De France, Marino Canal — Home feat. Delhia De France (Mind Against Remix)
**BPM**: 120 | **Duration**: 7:12 | **Key**: 120 BPM melodic techno

## Creative Brief

Mind Against's aesthetic is "celestial techno" — dark, hypnotic, spiralling, with a vintage warmth underneath cold atmospherics. Adriatique brings cinematic depth and emotional warmth. The remix sits at the collision: cold space with emotional longing. The word "Home" = reaching for warmth in a cold world.

**Visual philosophy**: Afterlife-inspired — dark base, monochromatic, selective color reveals, haze-dependent beam work. NOT a colorful show. Restraint is the design language.

**Rule**: Warm colors (amber/gold) appear ONLY when vocal energy is present. Otherwise, stay cold.

## Fixture Roles

- **Sharpy**: Lead beam — slow sweeps in cool blue, amber reveals on vocal peaks
- **BSW**: Supporting beam — mirrors/complements Sharpy, always blue/teal/white
- **Profile**: Front fill — subtle face light, teal wash, lower intensity than back movers
- **4BAR**: Atmosphere wash — slate blue default, warm amber during vocal sections
- **Missyees**: Accent wash — split cool/warm or unified blue
- **NI3K**: Texture — low-key blue LED, halo for depth. Lasers ONLY at Peak B climax

## Movement Strategy

- **Smooth crossfades only** — no snap changes except brief climax moment
- **4-bar minimum** crossfade during groove sections
- **8-bar crossfade** during breakdown
- Movers "breathe" (dimmer modulation) rather than strobe
- Breakdown: single mover, very slow diamond sweep
- Cross-beam effects for peak sections
- Reset to DSC between sections

## Section Details

### INTRO (0:00-0:43)
- Movers at center, dim, frosted beams
- 4BAR: slate blue wash
- NI3K: blue LED glow, no lasers, no halo
- Gradual dimmer increase over 22 bars

### FIRST WAVE (0:43-1:36)
- Movers begin slow L-R drift (SL → C → SR over 8 bars)
- Add violet undertone to pars
- Profile comes on with teal fill
- Energy dips slightly here — match with slightly lower dimmer

### PEAK A (1:36-3:12)
- Full mover movement — wider positions, cross-beams
- Warm amber reveals when vocal energy spikes
- BSW prism on for texture during high-energy moments
- NI3K halo blue, gentle tilt rotation
- 4BAR alternates slate blue / warm amber on 4-bar cycles

### BREAKDOWN (3:12-5:20)
- Strip to near-nothing over first 8 bars
- Single mover (Sharpy) with frosted beam, deep navy, very dim
- Slow diamond sweep: DSC → SR → USC → SL → DSC over 32 bars
- NI3K LED barely visible deep indigo glow
- All other fixtures dark
- Gradual rebuild in final 16 bars — add fixtures back one at a time

### PEAK B (5:20-6:40)
- "Coming home" — warmer than Peak A
- Amber + blue cross-beam pairing
- Full fixture involvement
- Brief strobe accent at climax (5:50 area)
- NI3K lasers on at highest energy moment, brief
- 4BAR warm amber wash

### OUTRO (6:40-7:17)
- Return to cold palette
- Progressive dimming
- Movers converge to center
- Final 8 bars: near-blackout, single blue glow fading
- End on blackout

## Key Techniques
- Dimmer modulation from energy envelopes (not snap changes)
- Vocal energy detection: warm color reveals
- Frost inversely proportional to energy (more energy = sharper beams)
- 4-8 bar scene changes with smooth crossfades
- Monochromatic restraint — warm colors are the "reward"
