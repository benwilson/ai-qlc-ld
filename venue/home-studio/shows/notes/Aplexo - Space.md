# Show Notes: Aplexo - Space

**Track**: Aplexo — Space
**BPM**: 125 | **Duration**: 3:48 | **Label**: Purified Records (Nora En Pure)

## Creative Brief

Purified Records aesthetic — deep, groove-forward, emotionally textured melodic house/techno.
Artwork by Jan Erik Waider (Northlandscapes): cold Nordic glacial landscapes, near-monochrome,
icy whites and deep blues, fragile and transformative.

The track title "Space" and the shimmering fractured synths imply:
- Cosmic vastness with intimate moments
- Single beam isolation — a star in open space
- Prism scatter = the "fractured synths" visual equivalent
- No warm colors — this is a cold-temperature palette throughout

## Fixture Roles

- **Sharpy**: Lead beam — the "star in space." Single tight beam, isolated against dark wash.
  Icy cyan or cold white. Prism + slow gobo during drops for fractured synth feel.
- **BSW**: Supporting atmosphere — frosted blue wash, not fighting Sharpy. Adds prism texture.
- **Profile**: Subtle fill — cold white/teal, very low intensity, almost subliminal.
- **4BAR**: Deep navy wash throughout. Icy cyan only at peak moments.
- **Missyees**: Near-black or very dim blue — texture only, not competing with movers.
- **NI3K**: Deep blue LED glow. Lasers ONLY at Drop 2 climax. Halo off until peak.

## Movement Strategy

- 4-bar smooth crossfades for intro/rebuild
- 2-bar crossfades during drops
- Breakdown: single Sharpy solo, very slow diamond sweep (8-bar crossfades)
- "Star in space" moments: Sharpy isolated at CEIL or DJ for cosmic feel
- No strobes (the fractured quality comes from prism/gobo, not strobe)
- Cross-beam positions at peak: Sharpy high (CEIL), BSW forward (DSC)

## Section Details

### INTRO (0:00-0:38)
- Start dark. Deep navy 4BAR, no other fixtures.
- Sharpy comes on at bar 8, aimed at center ceiling, barely visible
- BSW fades in at bar 12, frosted blue wash
- Gradual dimmer rise — like eyes adjusting to space

### DROP_1 (0:38-1:08)
- Sub arrives — increase all dimmers, sharpen beams
- Prism on Sharpy + BSW for fracturing synth texture
- Slow gobo rotation on BSW
- Movers sweep: DSC → SR → C → SL → DSC (water-like arc)
- NI3K: pale blue LED, halo blue

### BREAKDOWN (1:08-2:01)
- Strip back over first 12 bars
- 1:31 near-silence: Sharpy solo only, pointed at CEIL, dim=30, deep navy
- Very slow diamond sweep over the silence (8-bar crossfades)
- NI3K: dim blue glow only, no halo

### REBUILD (2:01-2:32)
- Add BSW back first, frosted
- Profile returns at bar 8 of rebuild
- 4BAR rises: deep navy → icy cyan at end of section
- Tension building — frost decreasing, beams sharpening

### DROP_2 (2:32-3:33)
- Full engagement. Icy cyan + cold white palette
- Wider positions, stronger prism
- Sharpy oscillates between CEIL and floor positions
- NI3K lasers at absolute peak (2:47-3:18)
- No strobe — the fractured quality comes from prism rotation

### OUTRO (3:33-3:54)
- Rapid dimming, all movers to center
- NI3K lasers off immediately
- Final state: faint Sharpy at C, deep navy, fade to blackout
