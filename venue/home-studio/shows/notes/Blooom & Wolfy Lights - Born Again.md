# Research Brief: Blooom & Wolfy Lights - Born Again

Status: `approved`

## Artist Branding
- Summary: Blooom and Wolfy Lights read as melodic, emotionally direct electronic artists: cinematic builds, vocal-forward warmth, and modern pop-leaning euphoric release rather than industrial darkness.
- Visual cues:
  - Introvert-to-euphoric intensity ramps with clear phrase contrast
  - Clean melodic beam lanes with emotional color lifts on hooks
  - Contemporary polished palette treatment with restrained saturation
- Do not copy:
  - Avoid copying exact social promo edit timing, cover-art compositions, or one-to-one choreography from official clips.

## Song Title Inspiration
- Keywords: born, again, renewal, ascent
- Motifs:
  - Geometry motif: center-locked starts that gradually expand into full-room orbits
  - Color motif: midnight blue and violet foundation opening into teal, amber, and warm white
  - Movement motif: longer fades in introspective phrases and tighter rhythmic pushes in chorus peaks

## Creative Thesis
- Build a rebirth arc that rises from dark introspection to luminous uplift, using progressive widening geometry and warm-white confirmations to make the title feel earned.

## Visual Direction
- Color story: Start with deep cool tones, add hopeful amber in transitions, and reserve warm white for the clearest rebirth moments.
- Motion story: Use gradual kinetic growth across sections: contained center breathing first, then broader and faster lateral travel in high-energy phrases.
- Staging story: Let PAR layers carry emotional bed color while movers narrate transformation through repeated returns to a brighter center state.

## Style Constraints
- Must include:
  - At least one low-density pre-rise section before first major release
  - Distinct phrase-level contrast between verse and chorus/drop families
  - A visible late-show brightening payoff that justifies the rebirth concept
- Avoid:
  - Constant maximum-output treatment from the start
  - Unbroken strobe walls that hide melodic phrasing
  - Random color cycling that ignores narrative progression

## Brand Alignment
- Score: 4.3 / 5
