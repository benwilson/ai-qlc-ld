# Show Notes: CamelPhat & Josh Gigante - The One

**Track**: CamelPhat & Josh Gigante — The One
**BPM**: 125 | **Duration**: 3:38 | **Label**: When Stars Align

## Creative Brief

CamelPhat's dark warehouse aesthetic meets Josh Gigante's Afterlife/Diynamic influence.
"Dark Matter" era: charcoal base, cold blues, electric teal, purple transitions.
Warm amber/gold at emotional peaks — the "earned" payoff.
Controlled cinematic journey. Not a rave strobe-fest.

## Color Palette

| Name | RGB | Use |

**Rule**: Cold for instrumental. Amber/warm for vocal. White for absolute peak.

## Wide Coverage Movement Strategy

Key technique: **split positions** — Sharpy and BSW aim at DIFFERENT parts of the room simultaneously.
This doubles coverage and creates dramatic spatial contrast.

| Split Name | Sharpy | BSW | Effect |
|-----------|--------|-----|--------|
| HIGH_LOW | CEIL | DSC | One up, one down — maximum depth |
| SPREAD | SL | SR | Full width — covers entire stage |
| DEEP | USC | DSC | Front-back depth contrast |
| DIAG_L | DSR | USL | Diagonal cross left |
| DIAG_R | DSL | USR | Diagonal cross right |
| FAR_L | DSC | SL | Forward + left spread |
| FAR_R | DSC | SR | Forward + right spread |
| CEILING_DJ | CEIL | DJ | Up high + DJ booth |
| X | SR side | SL side | Pre-defined cross |

Each section uses a DIFFERENT sweep path hitting different parts of the room:
- INTRO: Tight center → moderate spread
- STRIP: Wide sweeping L-R with ceiling hits
- PEAK_1: All corners in rotation — DSL, DSR, CEIL, X, USC, C
- BREAKDOWN: Solo sweeps (Sharpy alone through all positions)
- BUILD: Converge from spread to cross-beam
- PEAK_2: Maximum coverage, all split positions cycling, wider than PEAK_1
- OUTRO: Rapid convergence to center

## Fixture Roles

- **Sharpy**: Primary lead beam — most dynamic movement, tight beam, prism at peaks
- **BSW**: Secondary beam — often split from Sharpy for wide coverage
- **Profile**: Front-facing fill — slow independent movement, not following movers
- **4BAR**: Dark backdrop wash — charcoal/cold blue, amber at peaks
- **Missyees**: Deep violet/teal accent — pair with par color
- **NI3K**: Atmospheric LED + lasers at PEAK_2 climax
