# Show Notes: Claptone & Sea Girls - Put Your Love On Me (feat. Henry Camamile) [Korolova Extended Mix]

**Track**: Claptone & Sea Girls — Put Your Love On Me (feat. Henry Camamile) [Korolova Extended Mix]
**BPM**: 125 | **Duration**: 5:19 | **Label**: When Stars Align

## Creative Brief

Three distinct aesthetic signatures collide here:

**Claptone** — the golden plague-doctor mask, "The Masquerade" event series. Venetian ball
aesthetic: opulent, theatrical, built on shadows. His visual arc ("Immortal Live") starts
near-monochrome and slowly gains color — a reveal that feels earned. Amber/gold is the hero
color. Deep purple/violet is the masquerade's luxury accent.

**Korolova** — melodic techno with a cinematic patience. Golden-hour-over-water emotional
build. Deliberate, unhurried. The energy rises slowly, each section a careful step forward.

**Sea Girls** — anthemic indie pop vocal warmth. The vocal hook is the emotional peak.

Combined direction: **Start near-monochrome (amber/gold only), let color bloom on peak.**
The warm white climax at 4:03 is the earned "color bloom" moment — everything opens up at once.

User modifier: **"lots of beams with slightly faster sweeps"**
- All movers active simultaneously throughout (no solo/single-mover scenes)
- 1-bar crossfades during drops/climax; 2-bar during verses/builds
- SPLIT positions: Sharpy and BSW aim at different parts of the room simultaneously

## Fixture Roles

- **Sharpy**: Lead beam — amber/gold throughout, white only at climax. Prism at drops.
- **BSW**: Splits from Sharpy using SPLIT positions for wide coverage. Matches Sharpy color family.
- **Profile**: Gentle fill — follows mover positions. Low intensity (sub-dominant).
- **4BAR**: Par wash atmosphere. Tracks the palette arc: dark ember → amber → gold → warm white → back.
- **Missyees**: Dark accent — slightly dimmer than 4BAR. Purple accent at verse 2 / chorus 2.
- **NI3K**: Ambient amber glow through most of show. Warm white + lasers only at CLIMAX.

## Section Notes

### INTRO (0:00-0:45)
Near-dark start. Masquerade: the room is dim, a golden glow emerges from the haze.
2-bar crossfades, frosted beams start soft then sharpen.
Position sequence visits all quadrants — spatial depth builds gradually.

### VERSE_1 & VERSE_2 (vocal sections)
Vocal moments get slightly brighter pars. Verse 2 introduces purple to pars/missyees.
Gentle movement, 2-bar crossfades — the Korolova patience.

### CHORUS_1 & CHORUS_2
More active positions, faster cycling through SPLIT options.
Chorus 2 adds prism to both movers, purple in pars — the masquerade richens.

### DROP_1 (1:46-2:17)
Pure amber/gold. 1-bar crossfades. All 16 SPLIT positions cycle through.
The masquerade is in full swing — gold beams sweep every corner of the room.

### POST_DROP (2:17-2:32)
Sustained drop energy. Purple begins bleeding into pars as the masquerade deepens.

### DROP_2 (3:33-4:03)
Gold/purple interleave on odd/even steps. Maximum SPLIT coverage.
Sharpy alternates AMBER/PURPLE color wheel, BSW matches.
The "almost at peak" moment — rich, complex, anticipatory.

### CLIMAX (4:03-4:34)
The earned color bloom. Warm white everywhere. NI3K lasers on.
Prism spinning on all movers. Profile at full. All fixtures maximum output.
16 SPLIT positions cycle at 1-bar — the fastest, widest coverage of the show.

### OUTRO (4:34-5:20)
Palette reverse: warm white → soft gold → full amber → dark ember.
Frost rises back (beams soften). Dimmer scale decreases toward black.
NI3K lasers off from first step.
