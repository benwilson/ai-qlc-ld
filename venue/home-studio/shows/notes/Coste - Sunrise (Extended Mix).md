# Show Notes: Coste - Sunrise (Extended Mix)

## Track Info
- **Artist**: Coste
- **Track**: Sunrise (Extended Mix)
- **BPM**: 122
- **Duration**: 4:44 (283s, 144 bars)
- **Genre**: Melodic House / Progressive House

## Creative Brief

The title IS the concept. This show is literally a sunrise — every lighting decision maps to
a moment in that arc. We start in pre-dawn indigo darkness, warm amber bleeds in from one
side as the sun approaches the horizon, the first drop is the sun breaking through, the peak
is full golden daylight, the breakdown is a cloud shadow passing over the sun, and the second
drop is the real — larger — sunrise that burns through.

**Color arc**: Deep indigo/violet → amber bleeding in → warm orange → gold/white peak →
cloud-shadow violet → amber re-emerging → saturated gold with prisms → amber glow fading back.

Coste makes progressive/melodic house with rich melodic layering and patient builds.
"Sunrise" has a warm, optimistic, uplifting character — no aggression, all warmth.

## Mover Innovations (Distinct from Other Shows)

### 1. Profile Arc (Literal Rising Sun)
The Profile starts at the **Center Ceiling** position (tilt=30, aimed nearly straight up) and
physically descends to DSC (tilt=145) over the intro+build sections. When the drop hits, the
"sun" is at floor level. During the outro, it rises back toward the ceiling. This is a
**physical narrative** no other show in the rig uses — the mover arc IS the story.

### 2. Counter-Sweep (Horizon Widening)
Sharpy and BSW always sweep in **opposite directions**. When Sharpy goes SL, BSW goes SR.
When Sharpy goes DSR, BSW goes DSL. This "horizon widening" or "spreading wings" effect is
the opposite of most shows where movers converge/follow each other. It makes the room feel
like it's expanding outward as the energy rises.

### 3. Spinning Color Wheels During Build
Both movers spin their color wheels in opposite directions during the build phase:
- Sharpy: CW rotation (colormacro=180)
- BSW: CCW rotation (color=160)
This creates atmospheric "light scattering through morning atmosphere" — pre-sunrise
chromatic diffraction. Only appears in this one show.

### 4. No Lasers Until Drop 2
Lasers = **daylight breaking fully**. Red laser only at first Drop 2 arrival, red+green at
convergence, all 3 at Drop 2 inst peak. In other shows lasers appear much earlier. Here they
feel earned — the full sun has arrived.

### 5. BSW Heavy Frost During Atmospheric Sections
BSW frost (ch12 ≥ 129 for actual frost effect) used extensively in intro and break sections
to create a soft wash quality — morning haze diffusing the light. Other shows tend to use
tight beams throughout. This gives breakdowns a visually distinct "soft" texture.

## Key Techniques

- **Progressive reveal**: Every section adds one element. Verse = amber. Build = spinning.
  Drop 1 = counter-sweep. Peak = gold. Drop 2 = prisms. Drop 2P = all lasers. Never throws
  everything at once — the escalation has clear steps.
- **Profile as the clock**: Watching the Profile move from ceiling to floor and back
  is a physical clock that tells you where you are in the song's story.
- **Asymmetric warmth in verse**: Sharpy is warm (amber), BSW stays cool (violet) during
  the verse. This captures the moment of first light on one horizon while the sky on the
  other side is still dark. Pure sunrise physics.
- **Frost in breaks**: BSW frost≥130 during breakdowns creates soft diffused light
  (morning haze) vs. the tight crisp beams of drops (harsh midday sun).
