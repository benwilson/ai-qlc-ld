Status: `approved`

## Artist Branding
- Summary: WhoMadeWho-associated aesthetics typically emphasize emotive art-house electronic storytelling: elegant minimal foundations, human vocal texture, and carefully staged crescendos with premium cinematic polish.
- Visual cues:
  - Artful minimal openings that prioritize atmosphere and emotional clarity
  - Warm-meets-cool palette interplay with refined saturation control
  - Intentional pacing where large moments are earned through restraint
- Do not copy:
  - Avoid direct imitation of signature scenography, video installations, or specific festival production identities tied to the artists.

## Song Title Inspiration
- Keywords: all, love, embrace, connection
- Motifs:
  - Geometry motif: alternating intimate center states and wide inclusive arcs
  - Color motif: muted nocturnal base opening into warm amber, blush, and soft white affirmations
  - Movement motif: measured phrase breathing punctuated by emotionally timed expansions

## Creative Thesis
- Present a warm artful club narrative where intimate restraint and cinematic expansion trade off to embody “all the love” as both personal and communal space.

## Visual Direction
- Color story: Hold restrained dark-cool tones early, then progressively fold in warm amber and soft white for connective chorus statements.
- Motion story: Use smooth medium-length transitions for narrative coherence, with selected high-energy phrases receiving tighter rhythmic travel.
- Staging story: Let PAR fields establish emotional bed and use movers as expressive call-and-response voices that widen on communal payoff moments.

## Style Constraints
- Must include:
  - At least one intentionally intimate low-density section before major expansion
  - Visible warm-emotion payoff moments aligned to title/hook intent
  - Phrase-aware technique planning with explicit section contrast
- Avoid:
  - Relentless maximal intensity that removes emotional contour
  - Harsh strobe-forward treatment across vocal-driven passages
  - Incoherent per-bar motif resets that undermine cinematic flow
