# Research Brief: Fade Away (Extended Mix)

Status: `approved`

## Artist Branding
- Summary: Massano and braev align with dark melodic club language: cinematic pressure, precise build pacing, and emotionally restrained peaks that favor atmosphere over constant maximal output.
- Visual cues:
  - Cold-toned tension beds with measured intensity ramps
  - Long phrase transitions that preserve narrative momentum
  - Selective white accents reserved for structural payoff
- Do not copy:
  - Do not clone exact touring stage architecture, branded visuals, or recognizable cue-for-cue set pieces from official performances.

## Song Title Inspiration
- Keywords: fade, away, dissolve, release
- Motifs:
  - Geometry motif: center locks that blur outward into wider ring coverage
  - Color motif: graphite-blue and violet body with sparse silver-white release moments
  - Movement motif: slow decays and delayed returns, then tighter bar-grid accents in peaks

## Creative Thesis
- Shape the show as a controlled disappearance arc: tense center-held darkness gradually dissolves into wider cool beams so the feeling of fading away reads as intentional release, not collapse.

## Visual Direction
- Color story: Keep the base in dark blue-violet tones and let neutral white appear only when the phrase fully resolves.
- Motion story: Favor glide-first transitions in intros and breakdowns, then tighten movement cadence in drop-family bars.
- Staging story: Use PARs as the atmospheric glue while movers narrate the fade trajectory through repeated center-to-edge expansion.

## Style Constraints
- Must include:
  - At least one extended low-density phase before the first major release
  - Phrase-level contrast between suspended breakdown bars and drop bars
  - A late-track widening moment that clearly sells the fade-away concept
- Avoid:
  - Early full-bright treatment that removes narrative headroom
  - Constant high-rate strobe across melodic passages
  - Unrelated palette jumps that break the dissolve metaphor

## Brand Alignment
- Score: 4.3 / 5
