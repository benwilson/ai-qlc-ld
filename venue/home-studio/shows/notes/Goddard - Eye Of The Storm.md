# Show Notes: Goddard - Eye Of The Storm

## Creative Brief

DnB at 174 BPM. Genre: drum-and-bass. Moods: aggressive + building (peak for climax).

Storm as visual metaphor. Building mood creates gathering darkness and escalating tension (cool blues → warm reds). Aggressive mood unleashes the storm's fury on drops (red assault, heavy strobe, all fixtures). Peak mood provides the category-5 climax where every resource is deployed at maximum.

## Color Palette

Cool-to-hot progression following Building mood's temperature shift:

- **Deep Blue** — storm atmosphere, gathering energy, cool sections
- **Cyan** — electrical energy, mid-build transition, crackle before lightning
- **Red** — the storm's fury, drop impacts, aggressive peak
- **White** — lightning strikes, strobe impacts only, never sustained

## Mood Interactions Applied

### Building modifiers (dominant during intro/breakdowns/bridges):
- Progressive fixture addition (1 every 4-8 bars)
- Cool→warm color transition (blue → cyan → red)
- Speed 0.5x→1.5x accelerating, brightness 30%→90% ramping
- Strobe acceleration in final bars before each drop
- Pars start off, add one at a time

### Aggressive modifiers (dominant during drops):
- Speed 1.5x, instant snaps, 0.5x hold, heavy strobe
- Full lasers, all fixtures at peak, 120% energy scaling
- Blackouts as rhythmic weapon (1-beat accents)
- Red + white palette, maximum saturation, no frost

### Peak modifiers (climax section only):
- 120% energy, all fixtures, all lasers, all effects
- Internal dynamics: color swap every 4 bars, movement pattern shifts
- Brief blackout accents for punctuation
- Final 2 bars: full white whiteout

### Resolution:
- Intro/breakdowns: Building dominates (sparse→dense, cool→warm)
- Bridges: Building accelerates (faster fixture addition, strobe ramp)
- Drops: Aggressive dominates (red fury, all fixtures, strobe violence)
- Climax: Peak overrides all (maximum everything, category-5)

## Fixture Roles

- **Sharpy**: Lead beam — first mover to wake in builds, angular snaps on drops
- **BSW**: Mirror/follow Sharpy — cross-beams on drops, joins 2nd in builds
- **Profile**: Front accent — adds during builds, reinforces drop impacts
- **4BAR**: Progressive wash — off in breakdowns, adds during builds, full red on drops
- **Missyees**: Same progression as 4BAR — off → dim → full following build arc
- **NI3K**: Atmospheric anchor. Blue halo in calm, red halo + lasers on drops, all RGB lasers at peak. Heads rotate on drops, static otherwise.

## Section Breakdown

### 1. Gathering Storm (0:00-0:22, intro, 16 bars)
Building Phase 1: Gathering. Near-darkness → single blue beam → slow drift.
- 4 smooth 4-bar sweeps
- NI3K dim blue halo → Sharpy blue beam fades in
- Pars OFF entire section
- Storm gathering on the horizon

### 2. Storm Front (0:22-0:44, build, 16 bars)
Building Phase 2→3: Rising → Cresting. Fixtures add one by one.
- Sharpy (solo) → add BSW → add Profile → add pars
- Color: blue → cyan → red progression
- Movement accelerating from 4-bar smooth to 1-beat snaps
- Strobe acceleration last 4 bars, laser reveal last 2 bars

### 3. First Strike (0:44-1:17, drop 1, 24 bars)
Aggressive dominates. Red assault.
- Beat-level position cycling, angular snaps
- Red + white strobe bursts on downbeats
- NI3K red LED + red laser
- Pars: solid red at 60%
- 1-beat blackout accents every 4 bars
- Prism spinning for chaos

### 4. Eye of Storm (1:17-1:39, breakdown, 16 bars)
Building Phase 1 reset. Calm before next wave.
- SNAP to near-blackout
- Single blue beam stalking slowly (4-bar sweeps)
- Pars OFF, NI3K dim blue halo only
- Building starts gathering again in last 4 bars

### 5. Pressure Rising (1:39-1:55, bridge/build, 12 bars)
Building Phase 2→3 accelerated. Storm reforming faster.
- Faster fixture addition than Storm Front (2-bar intervals)
- Blue → cyan → red in 12 bars
- Strobe acceleration, laser reveal
- Everything primed for drop by final bar

### 6. Second Wave (1:55-2:17, drop 2, 16 bars)
Aggressive intensifies. Different texture from First Strike.
- Wider sweeps, more gobos
- All RGB NI3K lasers (vs red-only on Drop 1)
- Beat-level cycling, white strobe every 2 bars
- 1-beat blackout accents

### 7. Category 5 (2:17-2:39, climax, 16 bars)
Peak mood. Maximum everything. The storm's peak.
- All fixtures, all lasers, all effects
- Color swap every 4 bars: Red → White → Red → White (Peak internal dynamics)
- Position changes every beat, wide sweeps
- Brief blackout accents for rhythmic punctuation
- Last 2 bars: full white whiteout → snap to total blackout

### 8. Aftermath (snap to black)
One beat of silence. The storm is over.

## Key Techniques

- **Building→Aggressive contrast**: The progressive gathering of Building mood makes the instant Aggressive drop hits dramatically more impactful
- **Cool-to-hot arc**: Following Building mood's temperature shift creates a visual narrative from calm blue atmosphere to red fury
- **Peak escalation**: The Climax section's 120% energy and internal dynamics (4-bar color swaps) prevent fatigue while maintaining maximum impact
- **Fixture reservation**: Building sections deliberately hold back fixtures so their addition is felt — when all 7 return on drops, the impact is devastating
- **Storm pacing**: Building mood's patience (one element at a time) creates authentic storm-gathering tension
