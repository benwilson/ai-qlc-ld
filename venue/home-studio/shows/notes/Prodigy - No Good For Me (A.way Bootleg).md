# Show Notes: Prodigy - No Good For Me (A.way Bootleg)

## Creative Brief

Classic Prodigy rave energy reworked as DnB at 174 BPM. Genre: drum-and-bass. Moods: aggressive + dark.

The combined aesthetic: darkness as canvas, red violence as punctuation. Breakdowns are terrifyingly sparse (1-2 fixtures, near-blackout). Drops are sudden devastating assaults (all fixtures, red + white strobe). The extreme contrast between sections IS the show.

## Color Palette

Single-color-at-a-time approach (Dark mood monochromatic + Aggressive mood bold/simple):

- **Red** — THE color. Drops, builds, rolling. Full saturation, no compromise.
- **Deep Blue** — breakdowns only. Cold, oppressive, 1 fixture at a time.
- **White** — strobe impacts only, never sustained. Brief piercing moments.
- **Amber** — build transitions only. Warmth creeping in before the red assault.

## Mood Interactions Applied

### Aggressive modifiers (dominant during drops):
- Speed 1.5x, instant snaps, 0.5x hold, heavy strobe
- Full lasers, dual concepts, all fixtures at peak
- Blackouts as rhythmic weapon (1-beat blackout accents)
- 120% peak energy, no frost ever

### Dark modifiers (dominant during breakdowns/rolling):
- 30% base brightness, 1-2 fixtures active, pars off
- 1 color at a time, angular/deliberate movement
- 0.7x speed, 2x hold, stalking quality
- Static gobos for shadow texture, no prism rotation

### Resolution:
- Breakdowns: Dark dominates (near-blackout, 1 fixture, stalking)
- Builds: Dark→Aggressive transition (fixtures add one by one)
- Drops: Aggressive dominates (full assault, all fixtures, strobe violence)
- Rolling: Dark dominates (2 fixtures, single red beam, deliberate)

## Fixture Roles

- **Sharpy**: Lead beam — angular snaps on drops, single stalking beam on breakdowns
- **BSW**: Mirror/cross Sharpy — together they create devastating cross-beams
- **Profile**: Front accent — reinforces drop impacts, off during dark sections
- **4BAR**: Dim red backdrop on drops (40-60%), OFF during breakdowns (Dark mood)
- **Missyees**: Same as 4BAR — restrained, supporting role
- **NI3K**: Red halo architecture. Red laser as static cutting beam (Dark), all lasers on drops (Aggressive). Heads rotate on drops, static otherwise.

## Section Breakdown

Aggressive dominates. SNAP from darkness to red assault.
- Beat-level position cycling, angular snaps
- Red only, white strobe bursts on bar downbeats
- NI3K red LED + red laser

Dark→Aggressive transition.
- Phase A (4 bars): SNAP to near-blackout. 1 fixture stalking.

- Phase C (4 bars): Strobe build, laser reveal.

### 4. No Good (1:04-1:26, main drop, 16 bars)
Maximum Aggressive. THE peak. Dark Phase 3: Strike.

- All lasers on. NI3K heads rotating.

- 1-beat blackout accents for rhythmic assault.
- Pars: red at 60%.

### 5. Rolling (1:26-1:48, verse/groove, 16 bars)
Dark dominates. Controlled menace.
- 2 fixtures: Sharpy + NI3K only. Others off.
- Single deep red beam stalking slowly (smooth 2-bar sweeps).

### 6. The Hook (1:48-2:10, chorus, 16 bars)
Aggressive rising. 3-4 fixtures.
- Red with strobe accents every 4 bars.
- Movers angular, 2-beat repositioning.

### 7. Descent (2:10-2:32, breakdown→build, 16 bars)
Darkest point. Dark Phase 1: Stillness.
- Phase A (6 bars): Near-total blackout. Single blue beam.
- Phase B (6 bars): Build from darkness. Blue→red transition.
- Phase C (4 bars): Strobe build, laser reveal, all going red.

### 8. Blood Moon (2:32-2:54, drop 2, 16 bars)
Aggressive + deeper. Different texture from Drop 1.
- All RGB NI3K lasers. Wider sweeps.
- More gobos for textural complexity.
- 1-beat blackout accents.
- Pars: red at 60%.

### 9. Inferno (2:54-3:17, final, 16 bars)
Aggressive fully overrides Dark. No restraint.
- Everything on, maximum power.
- Red → all white last 2 bars.
- All lasers, strobe every bar.
- Snap to blackout.

## Key Techniques

- **Aggressive Dark contrast**: The extreme gap between near-blackout breakdowns and full-assault drops creates maximum emotional impact
- **Single color at a time**: Dark mood's monochromatic rule means red OR blue OR white, never mixed — bold and oppressive
- **Blackouts as weapon**: 1-beat blackouts during drops create rhythmic visual violence (Aggressive mood)
- **Stalking movement**: Dark mood's 0.7x speed + angular style during breakdowns creates menacing quality
- **Fixture reservation**: Rolling section strips to 2 fixtures — when all 7 return on the next drop, the impact is devastating
- **Laser architecture**: NI3K red laser as a static cutting beam during dark sections (not strobing), full RGB assault during drops
