# Show Notes: WhoMadeWho - Children (Mind Against & Dyzen Remix)

Status: `complete`

## Creative Brief
Afterlife unified beam organisms — all movers breathe as one living entity through
deep indigo darkness. Sunshine-to-moonbeams arc: warm gold on vocal moments, cool
silver-white on instrumental peaks. Single euphoric revelation at 3:56 — the only
moment of pure white in the entire show.

## Color Palette
- **Base**: Deep indigo (8, 5, 40) — near-black indigo, Afterlife darkness
- **Sunshine (vocals)**: Pale gold (160, 120, 50) / Sharpy Amber / BSW Yellow
- **Moonbeams (instrumental)**: Cool silver (120, 135, 160) / Sharpy White / BSW White
- **Climax only**: Reveal white (220, 200, 160) — warm white-gold, reserved for 3:56
- **Punctuation**: Blood mist (90, 8, 12) — desaturated red, used once at ~3:45
- **Avoid**: Saturated primaries, bright colors before climax

## Fixture Roles
- **Sharpy**: Lead beam — organism breathing, unified sweep. Indigo or gold wheel.
- **BSW**: Support beam — mirrors Sharpy position exactly (organism principle).
  Enters later than Sharpy in FIRST_WAVE. Indigo or gold wheel.
- **Profile**: "Distant star" in intro/outro (aimed at CEIL). Joins floor for CHOIR
  and later sections. Returns to CEIL in FADE_TO_STARS bookend.
- **4BAR**: Deep indigo atmosphere base. Pale gold on vocal moments (sunshine shift).
- **Missyees**: Minimal throughout — near-black accents. Pale gold only at REVELATION.
- **NI3K**: Subtle blue halo atmosphere. Lasers ONLY during LASER_PLATEAU (4:16-4:59).
  Tilt heads rotate during laser section for scanning effect.

## Positions
All movers target the SAME position (unified organism), except during SCATTERED:
- **PENDULUM sweep**: SL -> C -> SR -> C (4-bar per step = 16-bar cycle)
- **WIDE_SWEEP**: DSL -> C -> DSR -> C (plateau section)
- **SCATTERED positions**: Sharpy left zone, BSW right zone, Profile ceiling (only separation)

## Key Moments
- **0:14** — First vocal appearance: 4BAR breathes pale gold (sunshine hint)
- **0:45** — Sharpy enters: first visible beam, organism begins
- **1:54** — CHOIR: all movers join, warm gold full engagement
- **2:22** — SCATTERED: fixtures isolate (only time movers aren't unified)
- **3:45** — Blood-red punctuation: single dramatic moment before reveal
- **3:56** — REVELATION: pure white + gold, prism fracture, maximum brightness
- **4:16** — Lasers ON: NI3K scanning through indigo haze
- **4:59** — Lasers OFF: return to Profile at CEIL (distant star bookend)

## Afterlife Principles Applied
1. Unified organism: all movers same position, slow unison sweeps
2. Restraint: most of show at low-mid brightness, single peak at 3:56
3. No chase patterns: no independent mover movement (except breakdown)
4. No strobing: energy comes from color and movement, not flashing
5. Desaturated palette: even warm colors are muted (gold, not yellow)
6. Negative space: extensive darkness and shadow throughout
