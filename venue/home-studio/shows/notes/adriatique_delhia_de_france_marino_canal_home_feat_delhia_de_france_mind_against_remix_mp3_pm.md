  - Afterlife-like low-saturation scenes with haze-first beam architecture

## Creative Thesis
- Shape a restrained dark-to-warm journey where hypnotic blue geometry keeps the room in suspended tension until selective amber reunions express the idea of finding home.

## Visual Direction
- Color story: Open in steel-blue and deep navy darkness, hold cool monochrome for most phrases, and deploy amber or dusty gold only for emotional resolution moments.
- Motion story: Favor smooth 4-8 bar transitions with hypnotic circular travel during peaks and sparse single-source movement in breakdown valleys.
- Staging story: Keep the back movers as atmospheric narrator, let PAR wash define the room shell, and use center/downstage convergences as the recurring home anchor.

## Style Constraints
- Must include:
  - Warm palette moments only when phrasing or vocal energy justifies emotional payoff
  - At least one long-form 8-bar atmospheric sweep in the breakdown arc
  - Phrase-aware technique selection with explicit contrast on phrase boundaries
- Avoid:
  - Continuous full-rig strobe sections that flatten melodic phrasing
  - Rainbow cycling or saturated color changes every bar
  - Unmotivated random position jumps that break hypnotic continuity
