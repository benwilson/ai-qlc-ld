# Research Brief: Angara - Forgive You (feat. Ingrid Lukas) (Extended Version)

Status: `approved`

## Artist Branding
- Summary: Angara and Ingrid Lukas read as emotional melodic-house storytelling: intimate darkness, patient builds, and voice-led warmth emerging gradually from deep nocturnal palettes.
- Visual cues:
  - Water-like sweep paths and gentle long-arc transitions
  - Late-night blue/teal base with selective human warmth highlights
  - Bridge valleys that collapse intensity before earned euphoric reopenings
- Do not copy:
  - Do not imitate any single festival edit staging or signature one-to-one cueing from published live videos.

## Song Title Inspiration
- Keywords: forgive, release, peace, healing
- Motifs:
  - Geometry motif: inward-to-outward sweeps that widen as tension dissolves
  - Color motif: midnight blue to ocean teal, then amber and soft gold at emotional unlocks
  - Movement motif: patient 4-8 beat arcs in valleys, tighter 2-beat propulsion in drops

## Creative Thesis
- Translate forgiveness into light by evolving from dark river-blue tension toward warm amber release, using patient flow and widening space to signal emotional acceptance.

## Visual Direction
- Color story: Maintain deep blue and teal through intro and valley, then blend amber and soft gold as the vocal perspective resolves into release.
- Motion story: Design movement as flowing current: broad arcs during introspection, expanding width and pace only when sub and vocal confidence return.
- Staging story: Use center and DJ-focused intimacy in low sections, then spread to SL/SR/X/US zones during drop peaks to represent emotional opening.

## Style Constraints
- Must include:
  - At least one near-black bridge pocket with single-fixture emotional focus
  - Water-like mover route continuity rather than hard angular chopping
  - Drop sections must show measurable position-width expansion versus intro
- Avoid:
  - Aggressive nonstop strobe blocks that fight the vocal narrative
  - Cold-only palette in vocal release phrases
  - Busy per-bar motif switching that obscures long-form story

## Brand Alignment
- Score: 4.2 / 5
