# Research Brief: Aplexo - Space

Status: `approved`

## Artist Branding
- Summary: Aplexo on the Purified axis suggests melodic depth, polished atmosphere, and emotionally rich but controlled motion where texture and space matter more than blunt FX density.
- Visual cues:
  - Cold near-monochrome palette anchored in deep navy and icy cyan
  - Single-source beam isolation moments that read as stars in an open void
  - Prism and gobo texture used for fracture detail instead of strobe aggression
- Do not copy:
  - Avoid replicating identifiable label artwork compositions or exact scene choreography from official promo edits.

## Song Title Inspiration
- Keywords: space, void, orbit, distance
- Motifs:
  - Geometry motif: sparse center-to-ceiling routes with periodic wide-orbit expansions
  - Color motif: deep navy base, icy cyan accents, and cold white reserved for peak clarity
  - Movement motif: 4-beat drift in intro/breakdown with 2-beat pressure in drop phases

## Creative Thesis
- Treat the room as a cold cosmic void where isolated beam geometry and restrained cyan-white reveals convert fractured synth texture into spatial depth without warm-color contamination.

## Visual Direction
- Color story: Keep a strict cool-only progression from near-black navy into cyan and white, with no amber, red, or gold detours.
- Motion story: Frame low-energy sections as slow suspended drift, then tighten rhythm and widen coverage as sub energy and density increase.
- Staging story: Use Sharpy as the star narrator, keep BSW/Profile as atmospheric support, and let NI3K peak only at late drop climax.

## Style Constraints
- Must include:
  - Zero warm colors across all phrases
  - At least one extended low-density solo beam moment in breakdown
  - Prism texture in drop sections to represent fractured synth identity
- Avoid:
  - Amber, gold, or red accents in any section
  - Continuous rapid strobing used as a substitute for texture
  - Overfilling every bar with full-rig intensity in atmospheric passages

## Brand Alignment
- Score: 4.5 / 5
