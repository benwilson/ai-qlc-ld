# Research Brief: CamelPhat & Josh Gigante - The One

Status: `approved`

## Artist Branding
- Summary: CamelPhat and Josh Gigante point toward dark melodic club storytelling: disciplined low-key foundations, selective high-impact moments, and polished cinematic pacing rather than maximal constant effects.
- Visual cues:
  - Dark charcoal and cold-blue base with teal and violet tension layers
  - Wide split-position sweeps to create intentional spatial contrast
  - Amber and white deployed as earned peak language, not baseline color
- Do not copy:
  - Do not recreate exact branded stage packages, visuals, or known headline show cue structures.

## Song Title Inspiration
- Keywords: one, focus, gravity, convergence
- Motifs:
  - Geometry motif: split beams explore room extremes before converging on center anchors
  - Color motif: cold blue and charcoal body with amber/white as singular payoff tones
  - Movement motif: section-specific sweep routes with deliberate buildup from tight to full-span coverage

## Creative Thesis
- Deliver a controlled cinematic warehouse arc where split-position beam coverage and earned warm accents frame the title as a singular gravitational pull toward one shared center.

## Visual Direction
- Color story: Keep instrumental phrases in cold-blue, teal, and charcoal, then unlock amber and white only at emotional or structural peak milestones.
- Motion story: Use 2-bar and 1-bar transitions to increase urgency across sections while preserving coherent directional sweep narratives.
- Staging story: Treat Sharpy/BSW as dual protagonists in split coverage, with Profile and PAR layers reinforcing architecture and peak punctuation.

## Style Constraints
- Must include:
  - Frequent split-position sweeps where Sharpy and BSW target different zones
  - Clear section-level route variation so each act covers distinct room geometry
  - Peak white moments reserved for true climax only
- Avoid:
  - Flat same-position unison for entire sections in high-energy acts
  - Unbroken strobe-heavy blocks that collapse dynamic contrast
  - Warm color dominance during low-energy or stripped passages

## Brand Alignment
- Score: 4.4 / 5
