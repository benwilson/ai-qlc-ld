## Artist Branding
- Summary: The blend of Claptone theatricality, Korolova patience, and Sea Girls vocal warmth favors elegant dramatic pacing: opulent shadow-first scenes, gradual color bloom, and controlled peak release.
- Visual cues:
  - Masquerade warmth with amber and gold as primary identity tones
  - Purple luxury accents that enrich mid-to-late structural arcs
  - Earned warm-white bloom saved for true climax rather than early overexposure
- Do not copy:
  - Do not reproduce exact mask iconography sequences, branded event visuals, or one-to-one choreography from official stage captures.

## Song Title Inspiration
- Keywords: love, offering, devotion, bloom
- Motifs:
  - Geometry motif: broad split beams circling the room before converging into center declarations
  - Color motif: dark ember to amber and gold, then purple enrichment, then warm white bloom
  - Movement motif: all-mover active architecture with faster sweeps in drops and climax

## Creative Thesis
- Stage a masquerade-style reveal where warm monochrome gold evolves into rich amber-purple and finally blooms to warm white, mirroring the vocal plea in the title as an earned opening of light.

## Visual Direction
- Color story: Start near-black with ember tint, build through amber and gold dominance, layer purple in late build, and reserve warm white for the climactic bloom.
- Motion story: Maintain continuous beam activity with 2-bar transitions in narrative sections and 1-bar transitions for drops and climax propulsion.
- Staging story: Use split-position Sharpy/BSW pairings as core language while PAR and NI3K layers reinforce theatrical warmth and final bloom scale.

## Style Constraints
- Must include:
  - Warm palette family only; no cold cyan/blue world takeover
  - All movers active across key phrases with split coverage architecture
  - Climax must contain a distinct warm-white bloom moment with clear pre-bloom contrast
- Avoid:
  - Early full-white exposure that removes climax payoff
  - Cold-color detours that conflict with masquerade warmth direction
  - Overly static center-only blocking in drop sections
