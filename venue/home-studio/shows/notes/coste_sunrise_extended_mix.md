# Research Brief: Coste - Sunrise (Extended Mix)

Status: `approved`

## Artist Branding
- Summary: Coste-style melodic/progressive house reads as warm, uplifting, and patiently layered; the visual strategy should privilege long-form optimism over aggressive hard-edged shock moments.
- Visual cues:
  - Naturalistic dawn color progression from indigo to amber to gold-white
  - Patient build mechanics with physically legible movement arcs
  - Warm optimistic peak language supported by coherent geometric storytelling
- Do not copy:
  - Avoid copying exact sunrise visual edits or direct recreation of external show cue timelines.

## Song Title Inspiration
- Keywords: sunrise, dawn, horizon, daybreak
- Motifs:
  - Geometry motif: profile arc tracks sunrise elevation while Sharpy/BSW counter-sweeps widen the horizon
  - Color motif: indigo and violet pre-dawn to amber and warm gold full-day illumination
  - Movement motif: gradual reveal steps where each phrase adds one additional intensity or texture layer

## Creative Thesis
- Make the title literal by narrating dawn as a physical light journey: pre-dawn indigo, horizon amber bleed, and full-sun gold/white arrival with widening counter-sweeps.

## Visual Direction
- Color story: Progress from deep night indigo through first-light amber into saturated gold and warm-white climax, then taper back into soft dusk remnants.
- Motion story: Use long smooth transitions for intro/break and clearer directional drive in drops so sunrise acceleration is physically obvious.
- Staging story: Center profile trajectory functions as the sunrise clock while opposing Sharpy/BSW travel paints horizon expansion across the room.

## Style Constraints
- Must include:
  - A clear dawn progression with no abrupt cold-palette reset mid-story
  - Counter-sweep behavior where Sharpy and BSW often travel in opposite directions
  - Late-peak intensity escalation that feels earned by the preceding build
- Avoid:
  - Early peak blasting that removes sunrise narrative shape
  - Random palette swaps unrelated to dawn progression
  - Dense strobe-heavy treatments that break warm melodic tone

## Brand Alignment
- Score: 4.5 / 5
