# Research Brief: Daniel Portman - Ivory (Extended Mix)

Status: `approved`

## Artist Branding
- Summary: Daniel Portman visuals align with polished progressive-house control: driving but elegant phrasing, symmetric room architecture, and careful escalation over long form rather than chaotic effect spam.
- Visual cues:
  - Clean lane-based geometry with recurring center/downstage anchors
  - Dark foundational states supporting selective bright ivory revelations
  - Phrase-disciplined pacing where contrast is structural, not random
- Do not copy:
  - Do not duplicate exact cover design assets, promo typography, or one-to-one cueing from official campaign videos.

## Song Title Inspiration
- Keywords: ivory, stone, polish, luminous
- Motifs:
  - Geometry motif: column-like beam stacks and mirrored routes that lock to center phrases
  - Color motif: steel-indigo body with ivory-white crests and restrained amber warmth
  - Movement motif: 4-beat quantized repositioning with inclusion bursts during peak phrases

## Creative Thesis
- Express ivory as disciplined luminous architecture: dark progressive foundations carve space for polished warm-white statements and measured geometric returns.

## Visual Direction
- Color story: Begin in deep indigo and teal-steel tones, then graduate into ivory-white peaks with occasional amber support to preserve warmth and depth.
- Motion story: Maintain bar-quantized movement discipline while increasing route complexity and speed in build/drop transitions.
- Staging story: PAR layers provide structural bedrock while mover lanes narrate the stone-to-light journey through repeated geometric callbacks.

## Style Constraints
- Must include:
  - Bar-quantized mover routing with repeatable geometric signatures
  - Distinct ivory-white payoff zones contrasted against darker phrase beds
  - At least one inclusion relationship phrase where PAR pulse and mover phrase interlock
- Avoid:
  - Extended rainbow effects that conflict with ivory identity
  - Overdense strobe usage that obscures melodic phrasing
  - Frequent motif resets that break long-form progressive coherence

## Brand Alignment
- Score: 4.4 / 5
