# Research Brief: Daniel Portman - Loyalty (Extended Mix)

Status: `approved`

## Artist Branding
- Summary: Long-form progressive-house identity with disciplined, emotional club drive and clean high-control visuals.
- Visual cues:
  - Dark base plus deliberate contrast accents
  - Symmetric beam lanes and reconverging geometry
  - Palette worlds held for phrase readability
- Do not copy:
  - Exact artwork/typography/promo animation from official release assets

## Song Title Inspiration
- Keywords: loyalty, trust, return, commitment
- Motifs:
  - Geometry: mirrored paths that reunite at center
  - Color: steel-indigo base with restrained amber promises and white confirms
  - Motion: 4-beat lockstep returns as the recurring signature

## Creative Thesis
- Build a disciplined progressive-house arc where synchronized PAR+mover lockstep and recurring center returns embody loyalty, trust, and long-form momentum.
