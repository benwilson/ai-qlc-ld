# Research Brief: David Guetta & Sia - Beautiful People (Extended)

Status: `approved`

## Artist Branding
- Summary: David Guetta and Sia map to mainstream festival-pop dance language: immediate hook clarity, strong rise-and-release dynamics, and emotional singalong moments that need readable large-scale lighting.
- Visual cues:
  - Big-room symmetry and broad audience-facing sweeps
  - High-contrast chorus lift moments with cleaner whites and warm accents
  - Confident kinetic pacing that supports anthem-style vocal delivery
- Do not copy:
  - Do not reproduce exact choreographic signatures, branded stage designs, or direct re-creation of known tour cue stacks.

## Song Title Inspiration
- Keywords: beautiful, people, together, celebration
- Motifs:
  - Geometry motif: inclusive full-room fan-outs that repeatedly regroup at center
  - Color motif: vibrant cool base with warm-gold and white chorus affirmation
  - Movement motif: energetic bar-quantized sweeps with clearer punch in chorus/drop phrases

## Creative Thesis
- Frame the song as a high-energy anthem where bold crowd-wide coverage and bright chorus peaks celebrate collective identity, with disciplined contrast to keep each hook impactful.

## Visual Direction
- Color story: Use cool-to-warm interplay for momentum, then emphasize white and gold in hook peaks for anthem readability.
- Motion story: Keep movement assertive and legible: medium-fast directional transitions in high-energy sections, with brief decompression in verse resets.
- Staging story: Treat PARs as crowd glue and movers as chorus amplifiers; prioritize audience-facing presence in peak lines without flattening dynamic range.

## Style Constraints
- Must include:
  - Clear hook-level visual lift distinct from surrounding bars
  - Wide room-coverage moments that read as collective celebration
  - Phrase-aware contrast enforcement between verse and chorus/drop
- Avoid:
  - Monochrome-only treatment through the full arrangement
  - Continuous high-speed strobe that obscures vocal intelligibility
  - Dense visual clutter that competes with lead-vocal moments

## Brand Alignment
- Score: 4.2 / 5
