# Research Brief: Haums - Just Be (Extended Mix)

Status: `approved`

## Artist Branding
- Summary: HAUMS reads as melodic-progressive dance with balanced warmth and drive: clean low-end propulsion, understated drama, and emotionally clear transitions rather than aggressive maximalism.
- Visual cues:
  - Steady hypnotic pulse with gradual phrase growth
  - Teal-blue and soft amber interplay for calm-to-confident movement
  - Minimal clutter and readable structure in mid-energy sections
- Do not copy:
  - Do not replicate exact promo visual edits, stage branding, or signature artist cue structure from official sets.

## Song Title Inspiration
- Keywords: just, be, presence, grounded
- Motifs:
  - Geometry motif: recurring center holds with gentle lateral breathing
  - Color motif: cool cyan base warmed by restrained amber confirmations
  - Movement motif: long phrase exhales followed by concise per-bar lift gestures

## Creative Thesis
- Treat the show as a mindful groove progression where stable spatial anchors and breathing fades create a grounded "just be" state before each controlled rise.

## Visual Direction
- Color story: Begin in meditative cool tones, fold in warm highlights at key phrase arrivals, and preserve white as occasional clarity punctuation.
- Motion story: Use mostly smooth transitions through introspective bars, then shift to tighter rhythmic movement when chorus/drop energy arrives.
- Staging story: Keep PARs stable for emotional bed while mover pairs alternate call-and-response to imply breathing space.

## Style Constraints
- Must include:
  - A clearly restrained opening section with low visual density
  - Phrase-aware expansion that never abandons calm center readability
  - At least one chorus-family section where warmth enters as earned contrast
- Avoid:
  - Hyperactive motion in every bar of the arrangement
  - Heavy strobe-led looks across contemplative or vocal-led phrases
  - Chaotic multi-palette jumps that disrupt the grounded concept

## Brand Alignment
- Score: 4.1 / 5
