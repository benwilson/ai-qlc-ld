- Summary: Marsh is associated with polished melodic-house storytelling: emotional depth, progressive layering, and cinematic long-form arrangement that rewards disciplined restraint before lift moments.
- Visual cues:
  - Wide atmospheric beam fields with clean melodic contour
  - Cool-space palette foundations with selective luminous highlights
  - Long-range phrase architecture that favors narrative continuity
- Do not copy:
  - Do not recreate exact visual identities, branded scenography, or one-to-one cue patterns from official Marsh live/tour content.

## Song Title Inspiration
- Keywords: another, planet, orbit, ascent
- Motifs:
  - Geometry motif: orbiting arcs that repeatedly depart center then re-lock on a new axis
  - Color motif: deep indigo and cyan atmosphere with occasional white-star punctures
  - Movement motif: slow rotational travel in verses and tighter thrust patterns in drop/chorus peaks

## Creative Thesis
- Build an orbital narrative where restrained terrestrial grooves evolve into expansive celestial sweeps, making "another planet" feel like a gradual departure from gravity.

## Visual Direction
- Color story: Anchor the show in nocturnal blue-cyan tones and use bright white like star events, only at structural apex points.
- Motion story: Favor smooth orbital movement early, then increase directional urgency as sections imply launch and transit.
- Staging story: Let PAR texture provide space ambience while movers and NI arcs define trajectory, distance, and return vectors.

## Style Constraints
- Must include:
  - At least one long atmospheric segment that reads as weightless drift
  - Distinct shift from grounded verse behavior to expansive chorus/drop behavior
  - A late-stage high-clarity moment that signifies arrival beyond the initial space
- Avoid:
  - Continuous front-facing blast looks that erase depth
  - Frequent random position hops without orbital logic
  - Overusing white so climax events lose meaning
